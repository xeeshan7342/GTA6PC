About GTA 6 on PC

GTA 6 is coming to PC

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. A PC version hasn't been released or officially announced yet, but a GamesBeat report says Rockstar is aiming for early 2027.

When it arrives, expect a native PC version from Rockstar, like GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 got, with PC graphics settings, mouse and keyboard controls and support for high refresh rate screens.

Coming soon, not out yet

There's no GTA 6 PC version you can play today. Any "download" claiming otherwise is fake.

Native PC version

Rockstar's official PC releases run natively, with no emulator in between.

~234 GB install (est.)

Not confirmed by Rockstar. The leaked Xbox build was reported at about 209 GB, so plan for well over 200 GB on an SSD.

GTA 6 PC FAQ

Is GTA 6 out on PC?

Not yet. GTA 6 is only out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The PC version is coming soon but hasn't been officially announced.

What does "native PC version" mean?

The game runs directly on PC hardware as a normal PC program, with nothing being emulated. Rockstar's official PC releases work this way. More on native vs emulated.

How much space will I need?

Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements yet. Our estimate is around 234 GB, based on the leaked Xbox build being about 209 GB. An NVMe SSD is a good idea.

Is this an official site?

No. This is an unofficial fan page and isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

When is it coming to PC?

There's no official date. A GamesBeat report says Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027. See the full release date breakdown.
