GTA 6 PC FAQ

What does "native PC build" mean?

The game runs directly on PC hardware as a normal PC program. Nothing is being emulated, so performance isn't lost to translating console code on the fly.

Which version is it based on?

The PS5 build, version 1.0.12.

How much space do I need?

Plan for 234 GB of free storage. An NVMe SSD will help the open world stream in smoothly.
