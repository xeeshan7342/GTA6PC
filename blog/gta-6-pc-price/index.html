How much will GTA 6 cost on PC?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 launched at a higher price than most games. PC pricing hasn't been announced, but Rockstar's history makes a good guess possible.

Short answerOn consoles, GTA 6 costs $79.99 (Standard) and $99.99 (Ultimate, digital only). PC pricing hasn't been announced, but Rockstar has usually priced PC versions the same as consoles. Don't buy GTA 6 PC keys anywhere right now, because they don't exist.

GTA 6 console prices

Rockstar confirmed GTA 6's pricing in June 2026, ahead of pre-orders. In the US, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S:

EditionPriceNotes
Standard$79.99Digital, or physical as a download code in a box (no disc)
Ultimate$99.99Digital only, includes extra content
Ultimate upgrade$20.00For Standard owners

Pre-orders came with a Vintage Vice City pack, and digital pre-orders also included a month of GTA+.

What will GTA 6 cost on PC?

Rockstar hasn't announced PC pricing. Its recent history points to the same price as consoles:

  • GTA V launched on PC in 2015 at $59.99, the same as the console versions.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on PC in 2019 at $59.99, again matching consoles.

If Rockstar follows that pattern, expect around $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate on PC, with regional prices varying. That's our expectation, not an announcement.

Will PC get extra content?

Rockstar's PC versions have often added features, like the Rockstar Editor in GTA V and extra graphics options in RDR2, without raising the price. Nothing is confirmed for GTA 6.

Don't buy GTA 6 PC keys yet

There is no GTA 6 PC key for sale anywhere. Rockstar hasn't opened PC pre-orders. Sites selling "GTA 6 PC keys" or "PC pre-order codes" are scams, and some are built to steal payment details.

When PC pre-orders open, they'll be on Rockstar's store, Steam and the Epic Games Store. See our guide to spotting fake GTA 6 PC downloads and stores.

Should you buy on console now or wait?

If you own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S and can't wait, the console version is available now. If you only game on PC, waiting is the only official option. We compare both in GTA 6: wait for PC or play on console?

Last updated November 24, 2026. We update this page when Rockstar announces anything new.
