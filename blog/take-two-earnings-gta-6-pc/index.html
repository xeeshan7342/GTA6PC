What Take-Two said about GTA 6, and PC, in its latest earnings

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, reported earnings ten days before GTA 6 launched. PC players were hoping for a hint. Here's what was said, and what wasn't.

Short answerOn November 9, 2026, Take-Two said GTA 6 pre-orders were tracking far above expectations and kept its full-year forecast of $8.0–8.2 billion in net bookings. It said nothing about a PC version.

The headline numbers

  • Q2 fiscal 2027 net bookings fell 18% to $1.60 billion, as players waited for GTA 6.
  • Full-year forecast stayed at $8.0 to $8.2 billion in net bookings, a level that depends heavily on GTA 6.
  • Take-Two's stock rose about 4% in after-hours trading.

What Strauss Zelnick said about GTA 6

CEO Strauss Zelnick said GTA 6 pre-orders were tracking far above the company's initial expectations, and later told CNBC that demand was vastly exceeding them. Take-Two still hasn't released official pre-order numbers. Zelnick also said the company didn't want to claim success before it happened.

What about PC?

Nothing. Take-Two's earnings materials and call coverage described GTA 6 as a PS5 and Xbox Series X|S release and gave no PC plans or dates.

That's normal for Take-Two. It rarely talks about future platforms before Rockstar is ready to announce them. The most specific PC information remains a GamesBeat report, from October, of a Q1 2027 target. See our release date page.

Why PC matters to Take-Two

GTA V sold for more than a decade, and its PC version was a big part of that, helped by mods and roleplay. A PC release in early 2027 would land in Take-Two's next fiscal year, giving GTA 6 a second big sales moment. That's one reason many analysts expect PC sooner rather than later.

When is the next earnings call?

Take-Two usually reports its fiscal third quarter in early February. That would be the first call after GTA 6's launch, and a likely moment for first sales figures and possibly PC news.
