Will GTA 6 come to PC Game Pass?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

PC players keep asking the same two questions about GTA 6: when is it coming to PC, and will it be on PC Game Pass? The first has no official answer yet. The second has a clearer one, and it is no, not at launch, and probably not for a long time after.

Short answerGTA 6 is not on Xbox Game Pass or PC Game Pass. Microsoft's store sells it outright at $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, with no Game Pass inclusion shown, and neither Microsoft, Sony nor Rockstar has announced any subscription release. There is also no PC version yet, so there is no PC Game Pass route at all. History points to a wait of years, not months, if it ever joins.

The short answer, with the evidence

Start with what the stores show, because that is the part nobody has to take on trust. The Xbox store lists Grand Theft Auto VI as a normal paid game at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, releasing November 19, 2026. There is no Game Pass branding on the listing and no "included with" language of any kind. Xbox's own GTA VI page answers the question directly: the game is not available with Xbox Game Pass. Rockstar Support's official material describes the same two purchase editions, digital or physical, with no subscription route mentioned. Microsoft has published no announcement placing GTA 6 in any Game Pass tier, on console, PC or cloud.

PlayStation Plus is the same story. Sony has announced no PlayStation Plus inclusion for GTA 6, on any tier. And one thing helps PC players specifically: there is no PC version to put on a subscription yet. Rockstar has announced no PC version, no PC store page, no PC price and no PC system requirements. So the PC Game Pass question really has two blockers, not one. There is no PC version, and there is no subscription deal.

You also do not need a subscription to play. GTA 6's story mode is a single-player experience, so there is no subscription requirement hiding behind the purchase on either console.

Why Take-Two says no to day-one subscriptions

Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick has argued for years, in public and on the record, that putting frontline games on a subscription service on launch day makes no economic sense for the company. Those remarks were not about GTA 6 specifically, but they describe exactly the kind of game GTA 6 is: a frontline release that sells tens of millions of copies at full price. Take-Two's own launch reporting described record sales, and a game selling at that scale has no need for a subscription debut to find its audience.

That is a company position, not a promise about one game, and positions can change. But it has been consistent across years, and nothing Microsoft, Rockstar or Take-Two has said since launch points the other way. Until one of the three announces an inclusion, the honest answer is that there is no Game Pass release coming, and no date for one.

What Rockstar's history says about timing

Rockstar's own history gives the realistic shape of a future Game Pass debut, and it is slow. Grand Theft Auto V launched in September 2013 and first reached Xbox Game Pass about six years later. It has returned several times since for limited windows, most recently in April 2025, and it left again on April 15, 2026. Red Dead Redemption 2 moved faster, taking about 18 months from its October 2018 launch to reach Game Pass.

The pattern is clear. A Rockstar game reaches a subscription long after launch, stays for a limited window, and leaves again. It does not arrive on day one, and it does not stay forever. Apply that pattern to GTA 6 and even the optimistic case, a Red Dead style wait of around 18 months, puts a debut well past the PC launch. The GTA V case points to years. Anyone giving you a month for GTA 6 on Game Pass is guessing, and the history says they are guessing wrong.

The PC Game Pass question is really two questions

PC Game Pass is a separate catalogue from console Game Pass, and a game has to exist on PC before it can join either. The first question is when the PC version arrives. A GamesBeat report from October 13, 2026 says Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it, so treat it as a report, not a date. Our GTA 6 PC release date page tracks that story in full.

The second question is whether Rockstar would put the PC version on PC Game Pass at launch. Nothing suggests it. Rockstar sells its PC games through its own launcher, Steam and Epic, all purchase stores, and a subscription debut would be a break from everything it has done on PC. The realistic sequence is a paid PC launch first, through the usual stores, and only then, years later, a possible limited subscription window. See our PC launch explainer for how that rollout is likely to look.

Cloud gaming will not get you there either

Microsoft added one wrinkle on November 10. GTA 6 is playable through Xbox Cloud Gaming from launch day, including on Xbox One, but Xbox Wire says you buy the game separately. Cloud play is not a way to get it through a subscription, and it works on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles, not on PC. There is no PC cloud route, and GeForce Now has no listing either, because there is no PC version to stream.

An October 2026 report claiming Xbox had won exclusive cloud streaming rights to GTA 6 was publicly disputed by an Xbox executive and remains unconfirmed. The safe way to read it: cloud streaming is a way to play a game you already bought on a console you own, not a back door into GTA 6 on PC.

The GTA+ confusion

One more thing trips people up. Digital pre-orders of GTA 6 came with one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's membership for GTA Online. That has led some players to assume GTA 6 is part of a subscription. It is not. Rockstar's GTA+ page names GTA Online and nothing else, and it does not mention GTA VI. GTA+ is a membership for the older online game, not a route into GTA 6, and it says nothing about PC Game Pass. If you want the prices that do exist, our GTA 6 PC price guide has them.

Quick answers

Is GTA 6 on Xbox Game Pass?

No. The Xbox store sells Grand Theft Auto VI at $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, and shows no Game Pass inclusion. Microsoft has announced no Game Pass release.

Is GTA 6 on PC Game Pass?

No. GTA 6 has no announced PC version, so it cannot be on PC Game Pass. There is no PC store page, price or system requirements.

Will GTA 6 ever come to Game Pass?

Possibly, years from now, but nothing has been announced. GTA V took about six years to reach Game Pass, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took about 18 months. Both stayed for limited windows and left again.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC through Xbox Cloud Gaming?

No. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams GTA 6 to Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles only, and only if you buy the game. There is no PC cloud route.

Is GTA 6 on PlayStation Plus?

No. Sony has announced no PlayStation Plus inclusion for GTA 6.
