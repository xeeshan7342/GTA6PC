Did Cyberleek release the GTA 6 executable?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

The hacker group Cyberleek said it would release the missing piece of its leaked GTA 6 Xbox build, the main executable, on November 23. Here's where things stand.

Short answerAs of November 23, 2026, there's no confirmed report that Cyberleek has released the GTA 6 executable. Even if it does, the build is unverified, running it on PC would still need major work, and downloading it is risky and illegal.

Quick recap

Around GTA 6's console launch, Cyberleek posted what it called a near-complete November 2026 Xbox build of the game, around 200 GB. It held back one file, the main executable, without which nothing can run. Full background in our leak explainer.

The crypto-token condition

Cyberleek tied the executable to the market value of its own crypto token. Reports differ on the details. One said the target was lowered to a $15 million market cap from a $30 million goal in October. Another said the group would release the file when a poll ended on Monday evening, CET, whether or not the target was hit. PC Gamer reported the group said it would release it on November 23 "no matter what."

Tying a leak to a token price is a big red flag. It suggests the hype is at least partly about pumping the token.

Has it happened?

At the time of writing, we haven't found any reliable report confirming the executable was released, or that the build is genuine. We'll update this page as soon as there's credible news.

What would change if it's released?

  • It still wouldn't be playable on PC. An Xbox executable doesn't run on Windows as-is. Projects like Leonida would need to recompile it, and Xbox-only system functions would need to be rebuilt.
  • Encryption could be cracked. The game's files are reported to be encrypted with a key tied to the executable.
  • A flood of fakes is guaranteed. Whatever happens, expect fake "GTA 6 PC exe" downloads loaded with malware.

Why you shouldn't download it

Leaked builds are pirated content and a favorite way to spread malware. Security firms have already found fake GTA 6 installers carrying password stealers and ransomware. Don't download anything claiming to be the GTA 6 executable or a working PC build.

The official PC version is reported for early 2027. See our release date page.
