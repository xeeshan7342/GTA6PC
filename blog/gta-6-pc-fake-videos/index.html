Fake GTA 6 PC videos: how to spot them

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Every week of the PC wait will bring a new 'GTA 6 running on PC' video. Three are already circulating. Here is what each one actually shows, and the checklist that would have caught them in a minute.

Short answerThe biggest viral 'PC port' video was recycled GTA V footage posted by a days-old account. The RTX 5080 clip is unverified, with no reproducible details. The rest are Remote Play streams from a console with an overlay added. No verified video of GTA 6 running on a PC exists.

Fake 1: the 'PC port' that was GTA V

An X account registered days before posting claimed its unofficial port of the PS5 version had reached the main menu and crashed on starting gameplay. Viewers who went frame by frame found the footage was GTA 5 running on PC. The account had no history, posted nothing else, and has not responded. Full breakdown in our PS5 build fact check.

Fake 2: the RTX 5080 clip

A Reddit clip, reported by GameGPU, shows a windowed game with a debug translator window and a counter reading 24 to 25 fps, said to be an unofficial port on Windows 11 with an RTX 5080. It omits the CPU, resolution, settings and driver, and nobody has reproduced it. A recording played in a window with overlays produces exactly this picture, which is why the outlets covering it label it unverified.

Fake 3: Remote Play with an overlay

The most common version is not even edited footage. A PS5 streams the real game to a PC with Remote Play, the creator adds a PC performance overlay, and sometimes frame generation on the video feed. It looks like a PC release and it is a console doing all the work, at console frame rates. See how Remote Play actually works.

The one-minute checklist

  • Source of the game: GTA 6 is not dumped, so any PC run needs an answer for where the copy came from.
  • Tracker check: is the result on the AnyPS5 tracker or the project's GitHub, or only in the video?
  • Reproduction: has anyone else, anywhere, got the same result?
  • Details: real benchmarks name the full hardware, settings, resolution and driver.
  • The ask: if the video ends with a download link, a key for sale or a Discord invite, it is a scam funnel.

Quick answers

Is the GTA 6 PC gameplay video real?

None verified so far is. The most shared port video was traced to GTA V footage, the RTX 5080 clip is unauthenticated, and streaming setups are consoles playing through a PC screen.

How can I check a GTA 6 PC video myself?

Ask where the game copy came from, whether the project tracker lists the result, and whether anyone else reproduced it. If the only evidence is the video itself, treat it as fake.

Why do fake GTA 6 PC videos spread so fast?

Because the background progress is real: PS5 games have started running natively on PC, so a GTA 6 claim sounds plausible and gets shared before anyone checks it.
