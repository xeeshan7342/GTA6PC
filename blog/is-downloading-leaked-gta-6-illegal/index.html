Is it illegal to download the leaked GTA 6 build?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

With the GTA 6 Xbox executable out and an early PC boot making headlines, lots of people are wondering whether they can just download it. Here's a plain-English look at the legal and practical risks.

Short answerIn most countries, downloading or sharing a leaked copy of GTA 6 is copyright infringement, because you don't have a license to it. Rockstar is actively moving against port projects and leak communities, and leaked files are a common way to spread malware. This isn't legal advice, but the safe answer is: don't.

This article is general information, not legal advice. Laws differ by country.

Why it's infringement

When you buy a game, you get a license to use it under the publisher's terms. A leaked build is a copy that was never licensed to anyone outside the company. In most countries, copying or downloading copyrighted software without permission is copyright infringement, and sharing it, for example by seeding a torrent, is usually treated more seriously than downloading.

Owning GTA 6 on a console doesn't change this. Your console license covers the console version from the official store, not a leaked build on a PC.

What about the port projects?

Writing your own code, like an emulator or a recompiler, isn't the same as distributing the game, and emulators are generally legal in many countries. But running GTA 6 through any of them still needs the game's files, and the only copies available on PC right now are leaked ones.

Rockstar has reportedly been moving against port projects and leak communities since the executable leak. The Leonida project shut down and several Discord servers were taken down. See our crackdown report.

The practical risks

  • Malware: security firms have found fake GTA 6 downloads carrying password stealers, remote access tools and ransomware. Leaks are the perfect cover for them. See our scam guide.
  • Notices from your internet provider: torrenting copyrighted files can lead to warning letters in some countries.
  • Nothing to play: even the much-shared PC boot only reached the settings screens, with no gameplay.

Is it illegal to read or watch leak coverage?

No. Reading news about the leak, or watching coverage of it, isn't the same as downloading the files. This site reports on the leak but never links to the files.

The legal ways to play

  • On PS5 or Xbox Series X|S now.
  • On a PC screen today, by streaming from your own console with Remote Play.
  • Natively on PC when Rockstar releases it, reported for early 2027. See our release date page.
