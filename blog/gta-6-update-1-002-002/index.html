What does GTA 6 update 1.002.002 fix?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

Four days after launch, GTA 6 has its first patch. Update 1.002.002 started rolling out on November 23, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, fixing seven issues players reported since launch day, including broken autosaves and phone calls that never triggered. There is no PC version to patch yet, but this update still matters for PC players.

Short answerRockstar released GTA 6 update 1.002.002 on November 23, 2026, the first patch since the November 19 launch. It fixes seven issues: crashes, weather that never changed, broken autosaves, phone calls that did not trigger, Wanted Levels that would not clear, a gym equipment bug and a fence-selling bug. It is 603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, and it is rolling out in phases. There is no PC version yet, so PC players get nothing to download, but the PC version should ship with these fixes already in.

What update 1.002.002 changes

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and players quickly reported a set of bugs, some of them able to block story progress. Rockstar answered fast. On November 23, the first post-launch patch, version 1.002.002 on PS5 and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox, started rolling out with seven confirmed fixes. The patch notes come from Rockstar's own support page, as reported by VGC, GameSpot and GameRant.

FixWhat it does
CrashesFixed several issues that caused the game to crash
WeatherFixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods
AutosaveFixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly
Phone callsFixed several issues that resulted in phone calls not triggering correctly, including calls that progress the story or unlock mission content
Wanted LevelFixed an issue that resulted in players being unable to lose their Wanted Level
GymFixed an issue that resulted in players being unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym
FencesFixed an issue that resulted in partners not handing over bags so players could sell them to fences

None of the seven fixes adds new content. This is a stability and progression patch, the kind Rockstar usually ships in the first days after a launch, and more patches are likely to follow. Our GTA 6 PC release date page tracks the bigger picture while the console version gets patched.

Download size and rollout

The update is small. On PlayStation 5 it is about 603.8 MB, and on Xbox Series X|S it is about 1.6 GB, according to download size reports cited by GAMINGbible. The size difference between platforms is normal: the same fixes can be packaged differently on each console.

The PS5 rollout is phased. Insider Gaming reported, citing PlayStation Game Size, that about a third of players could download the update first, with two thirds able to access it about three hours later and the rest about six hours after that. If the update has not appeared on your console yet, that is the rollout, not a problem with your game. The Xbox version rolled out the same day.

The autosave fix matters most

Of the seven fixes, the autosave repair is the one most players will notice. Some players found the game was not autosaving correctly, which meant lost progress after a crash or a closed session. That also fed a piece of misinformation online claiming GTA 6 had no autosave feature at all. That claim was not true: the game does autosave, and this patch fixes the cases where it failed. If you lost progress before the patch, the fix stops it happening again, but it cannot restore saves that were never written.

The phone call fix is nearly as important. Several calls that progress the story or unlock mission content were not triggering, which could leave players stuck with no obvious next step. The Wanted Level fix matters too: players reported being unable to lose their Wanted Level at all, which turns normal free-roam play into a permanent police chase.

What the patch does not fix

Not every reported problem is in this patch. GAMINGbible reports that players are still hitting a potentially game-breaking glitch after the update: completing the Missing Persons side mission in Kelly County during Chapter 3 can leave players stuck as Lucia, unable to switch back to Jason. At that point in the story you are required to play as Jason, so the glitch can block progress entirely. Rockstar has not listed it among the fixed issues, so if you are about to start that mission, it may be worth waiting for the next patch or keeping a manual save from before it.

That is a normal pattern for a launch week. The first patch takes out the most common and most reported problems, and later patches work through the longer list. Rockstar has not announced a date for the next update.

What this means for PC players

There is no PC version of GTA 6 yet, so there is nothing for PC players to download. The patch still matters for the PC wait in two ways. First, every fix Rockstar ships on console now becomes part of the base the PC version is built from. When the PC version eventually arrives, reported for the first quarter of 2027 but not confirmed, it should launch with these fixes already in, rather than repeating the same launch-week bugs. Second, the speed of this patch shows Rockstar is actively supporting the game, which is what PC players want to see before a port is announced.

Until then, the only GTA 6 on PC is unofficial. Our native build tracker follows the real projects trying to run the game on PC, and our download scams guide explains why every GTA 6 PC download offered today is fake. Do not download anything claiming to be this patch for PC. It does not exist.

Quick answers

What does GTA 6 update 1.002.002 fix?

Seven issues: several crash fixes, weather that did not change for extended periods, autosaves not working correctly, phone calls not triggering (including story and mission calls), Wanted Levels that could not be lost, the deadlift bar in the Gym not working, and partners not handing over bags to sell to fences.

How big is the GTA 6 1.002.002 update?

About 603.8 MB on PlayStation 5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. The PS5 update is rolling out in phases, so it may not appear for every player at the same time.

Is GTA 6 update 1.002.002 on PC?

No. There is no PC version of GTA 6 yet, so the patch is only for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version has been reported for the first quarter of 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Does update 1.002.002 fix the Missing Persons glitch?

No, based on player reports after the patch. Players completing the Missing Persons side mission in Kelly County during Chapter 3 can get stuck as Lucia, unable to switch back to Jason. That issue was still being reported after the update.

Will GTA 6 on PC include these fixes?

Almost certainly. The PC version has not been announced, but when it arrives it will be built from the current console version, which already includes this patch. Our release date page tracks the reported Q1 2027 window.
