GTA 6 sales: record pre-orders and what PC could add

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 was expected to be the biggest entertainment launch ever. Official sales numbers aren't out yet, but the forecasts and pre-order data give a good picture, and PC is a big part of the long-term story.

Short answerNewzoo forecast $3.25–5.2 billion in first-week revenue, around 51 million copies at the midpoint, after the strongest pre-order campaign on record. Official launch sales haven't been published yet. A PC release could add a second big wave.

Pre-orders broke records

Analytics firm Newzoo estimated GTA 6 brought in about $260 million in global digital pre-orders in its first week of pre-orders, the strongest pre-order campaign it had ever recorded. Take-Two later said pre-orders were tracking far above its expectations.

The first-week forecasts

  • Newzoo forecast $3.25 billion to $5.2 billion in first-week revenue.
  • Its midpoint was around $4.5 billion, or roughly 51 million copies.
  • For comparison, GTA V made about $1.15 billion in its first week in 2013.

These are forecasts made before launch, not reported results. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't published official launch-week sales yet. Take-Two's next earnings report, usually in early February, is the most likely place for them.

Why PC matters for sales

GTA V is the clearest example. It launched on PS3 and Xbox 360 in 2013, came to PS4, Xbox One and PC over the next two years, and went on to sell more than 200 million copies. The PC version, with mods and roleplay, kept it relevant for over a decade.

GTA 6 launched only on consoles. A PC release, reported for early 2027, would reach millions of players who don't own a PS5 or Xbox, and many console players would likely buy it again for mods and higher frame rates.

What to watch for

  • Official sales announcements from Rockstar or Take-Two.
  • Take-Two's February earnings, which could include sales and possibly PC news.
  • A PC announcement from Rockstar. Follow our release date page for that.
