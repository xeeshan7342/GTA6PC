GTA 6: wait for PC or play on console now?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 is out on consoles and PC players are stuck waiting. Is it worth buying a console version now, or should you hold out?

Short answerIf you already own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S and want to play now, buy it on console. If you only play on PC and can wait, the PC version is reported for early 2027 and Rockstar's PC versions usually add higher frame rates, more settings and mod support.

Play on console now if…

  • You already own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.
  • You don't want to avoid spoilers for months.
  • You're happy with console performance and settings.

GTA 6 costs $79.99 on console, or $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. See our price guide.

Wait for PC if…

  • You only game on PC and don't want to buy a console.
  • You care about high frame rates, ultrawide screens or mouse and keyboard.
  • You want mods or roleplay servers.

What Rockstar's PC versions usually add

ConsolePC (based on GTA V and RDR2)
Frame rateSet by Rockstar per console modeUnlocked, high refresh rate support
Graphics settingsA few modesDozens of options
ScreensTV, 16:9Ultrawide and multi-monitor
ControlsControllerController or mouse and keyboard
Mods and roleplayNoUsually yes
AvailableNowReported Q1 2027, not confirmed

Nothing on the PC side is confirmed for GTA 6 yet. It's the pattern from Rockstar's last two PC releases.

Can you buy on console and play on PC later?

Rockstar hasn't said whether GTA 6 purchases or progress will carry over to PC. When GTA V moved to PC, players had to buy it again, though GTA Online progress could be transferred. Plan on buying twice if you do both.

Our take

If you own a current console, play it now. The story will be spoiled everywhere long before PC arrives. If you're PC-only, the wait is likely a few months, not years, and you'll probably get the best-looking version of the game.

Last updated November 24, 2026. We update this page when Rockstar announces anything new.
