Best PC build for GTA 6, by budget

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

You asked for builds, so here are builds, with the warning up front: Rockstar has published no PC requirements, so every part below is an estimate from the PS5 and Xbox hardware, not a promise.

Short answerPlan around the console baseline: an 8-core Zen 2 class CPU, 16 GB of memory and a fast SSD. A sensible budget build pairs a modern 6-core CPU with an RTX 3060 class card, a mid-range build steps to an RTX 4070 class card for 1440p, and high-end means a current flagship card and 32 GB of RAM. Every build needs 250 GB or more of free NVMe SSD space.

Start from the console baseline

GTA 6 was built for machines with an 8-core Zen 2 CPU, 16 GB of unified memory and a very fast SSD, rendering at 1440p and 30 fps on PS5 and Series X. A PC build that wants to match or beat that experience should start at that level, not below it. The full reasoning is in our system requirements explainer.

These are planning tiers, not benchmarks. CPU demand is the big unknown: analysis of the console version suggests the world simulation is CPU heavy, which is why every tier keeps a strong, recent processor.

The upgrade order, if you are upgrading

  1. SSD space first. If you do not have 250 GB free on a fast NVMe drive, nothing else matters.
  2. RAM to 16 GB minimum. Cheap, and every modern game wants it.
  3. GPU next, for resolution and settings headroom.
  4. CPU last, and only when real requirements land, because it usually means a new motherboard too.

What not to do

Do not buy parts from a spec table on a site that also offers a GTA 6 download. Do not treat the unverified RTX 5080 clip as a benchmark, we checked it in the fake videos guide. And do not rush: if the Q1 2027 report is right, real requirements will be published before you need the hardware.

Quick answers

What PC do I need for GTA 6?

There are no official requirements. Based on the console hardware, a modern 6 to 8 core CPU, an RTX 3060 class graphics card or better, 16 GB of RAM and a fast SSD is a reasonable planning baseline.

Will a Ryzen 5 3600 and RTX 2060 run GTA 6 on PC?

Nobody can say until Rockstar publishes requirements and the game exists on PC. That pairing is below the console baseline on graphics memory, so expect to wait for real benchmarks before planning around it.

How much SSD space should I keep free?

Over 200 GB at minimum. The console install is over 200 GB and the leaked Xbox build was reported at about 209 GB, so 250 GB free is a safer plan.
