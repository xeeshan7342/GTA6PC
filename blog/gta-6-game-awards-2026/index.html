GTA 6 at The Game Awards 2026: eight nominations, one big PC question

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 28, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI leads The Game Awards 2026 with eight nominations, including Game of the Year, and it earned them before the game was even out. The nominees were announced on November 17, 2026, two days before the November 19 launch, and the winners are revealed live on December 10. For PC players, the show carries a second question: whether Rockstar uses gaming's biggest stage to say anything about a PC version, which it still has not announced.

Short answerGTA 6 has eight nominations at The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year, announced on November 17, two days before launch. The winners are revealed live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Thursday, December 10, 2026. The game is the favourite, with a 99 Metacritic score and an estimated 53 million copies sold in its first week. Rockstar has announced no PC version, and a PC announcement at the show is speculation, not a rumour with a source.

Eight nominations, announced before launch

Grand Theft Auto VI leads The Game Awards 2026 with eight nominations, including Game of the Year. Producer and host Geoff Keighley announced the nominees during a live stream on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, two days before the game launched on November 19, and a day before the review embargo lifted. VGC, which sits on the voting jury, reports that GTA 6 is up for eight awards in total, and GameSpot and Hypebeast confirm the same count.

The timing is unusual but allowed. The Game Awards' eligibility rules require games to be commercially available on or before Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 p.m. PT, and GTA 6 launched on November 19, so it squeezed in by a day. The jury of more than 100 global media and creator outlets had advance access, which is how a game nobody outside the press had played could lead the nominations before release.

The eight nominations are Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, Best Action/Adventure, and Best Performance for Manni L. Perez as Lucia. The performance nomination also served as the public confirmation that Perez plays Lucia, something Rockstar had not officially revealed. Jason's actor was not nominated, so that identity is still not officially revealed.

The Game of the Year race

Six games will contest the top prize, not the usual five, after a tie in the jury voting. The nominees are 007 First Light from IO Interactive, Control Resonant from Remedy and Annapurna, Grand Theft Auto VI from Rockstar Games, Phantom Blade Zero from S-GAME, Resident Evil Requiem from Capcom, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, Nintendo's remake of the 1998 classic.

GTA 6 is tied for the most nominated game overall, with eight nominations each for Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem. Phantom Blade Zero has five, and 007 First Light and the Zelda remake have four each. Nintendo is the most nominated publisher with 12 nominations, followed by Capcom with 11, while Rockstar Games has eight, all of them for GTA 6.

The winner is decided by a blended vote: the jury counts for 90 percent and the public vote for 10 percent. Fan voting is open at thegameawards.com until December 9 at 6 p.m. PT, and voters can update their saved ballots until the deadline.

Why GTA 6 is the favourite

The case for GTA 6 winning is the strongest any game has carried into the show in years. It holds a 99 Metacritic score, based on more than 150 critic reviews, all of them positive at launch, which ties the original 1998 Ocarina of Time for the highest score in the site's history. Push Square reports that it still sits at 99 with more than 160 reviews. IGN awarded it 10 out of 10, GameSpot awarded it 10 out of 10, and VGC gave it 5 out of 5.

The commercial case is just as strong. Alinea Analytics estimates GTA 6 sold 53 million copies in its first week, worth about $4.7 billion, and Take-Two's own official figure is $3.79 billion in day one retail sales. Our first week sales report has the full figures. No Game of the Year winner has ever arrived at the show with numbers like these.

There is one blemish on the record. The Metacritic user score sits at 5.4 out of 10, after review bombing by frustrated users, and Metacritic had to remove early ratings submitted before the game was officially available. The jury vote, which decides 90 percent of the result, is a critic vote, so the user score should not move the outcome.

When and where to watch

The Game Awards 2026 takes place on Thursday, December 10, 2026, live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. The show starts at 7:30 p.m. ET, or 4:30 p.m. PT, which is 00:30 UTC on Friday, December 11. It streams free on more than 40 platforms, including YouTube, Twitch, TikTok Live, X, Steam, Prime Video, and live TV on PS5.

The show is also the year's biggest stage for new announcements, with new trailers and reveals between the awards, and musical performances by The Game Awards Orchestra. That is why the date matters beyond the trophies, and why PC players have it circled.

The PC question on the night

Here is the honest position. Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6 and no PC release date, and no outlet has reported a PC announcement for The Game Awards. The GamesBeat report from October 2026, citing four anonymous sources, says Rockstar is aiming for the first quarter of 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it, and neither the November 9 Take-Two results nor the November 19 launch announcement mentions PC. Our PC release date status page has the full official record.

What is true is that December 10 is the kind of stage where a PC announcement would land hardest. Rockstar has a game with eight nominations, a 99 Metacritic score, and the biggest launch in entertainment history, and it will have the attention of every platform on the night. A PC date announced from that stage would reach more players than any blog post or trailer drop. That is an argument about the stage, not a report about Rockstar's plans, and it should be read that way.

What to watch instead is simpler. If Rockstar wins Game of the Year, the acceptance speech and the night's coverage will be the moment any PC news would surface. If the show passes with no mention of PC, nothing has changed: the GamesBeat report stays unconfirmed, and the next date to watch is Take-Two's next earnings release. A trophy does not move a release date, and a win on December 10 would be about the console game, not about PC.

What a win would and would not mean

A Game of the Year win would confirm what the reviews already say: GTA 6 is the defining game of 2026. It would not change anything about the PC version. The PC version is still unannounced, the unofficial ports are still the only route to a PC screen, and every GTA 6 PC download on the internet today is still fake. Our fake download warning explains the malware risk.

The PC story will move when Rockstar moves it, and the company has said nothing. Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick has said PC is becoming more and more important to the company, and that on a big title PC can account for 45 to 50 percent of sales, which is why the PC version matters so much. Until Rockstar announces it, the honest answer to every PC question is the same: no date, no listing, no pre-order, and no download. December 10 is the next night that could change that, and we will update this page when it does.
