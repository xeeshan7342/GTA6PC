GTA 6 PC in March 2027? The claim and the fake press release

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 26, 2026

Two different March 2027 claims are circulating about GTA 6 on PC, and they keep getting mixed together. One is a hardware site reading Take-Two's financial forecast. The other is a fake press release made with an AI tool and posted on X. Neither comes from Rockstar, and neither is an announcement. Here is how to tell them apart.

Short answerGameGPU claims GTA 6 arrives on PC in March 2027, with GTA 6 Online launching the same day, based on reading Take-Two's fiscal 2027 bookings forecast. A separate document dressed as an official Rockstar press release making the same claim is fake: it was posted on X on November 24, made with an AI tool, and debunked on November 25. Rockstar has announced no PC version and no Online date.

What the March 2027 claim actually says

On November 25, 2026, hardware and benchmarking site GameGPU published a post claiming GTA 6 will be released on PC in March 2027, along with the launch of GTA 6 Online on the same day, on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. The post adds several specific figures: a total development budget of 2.1 billion dollars, another 780 million dollars of infrastructure and content spending before the multiplayer launch, and 9.69 million pre-orders. It also describes the online mode as a single, permanent map called Leonidas, spanning Vice City, Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia, and says funds and progress from GTA 5 Online will not carry over, so all players start from scratch.

The same post lists the risks it sees in the launch: server stability with a much larger audience than the 2013 launch of GTA 5 Online, map crashes and critical mission errors in the base game, and the fact that the console version did not launch with a 60 frames per second performance mode. It also says the distribution model is unclear, and it is not yet known whether GTA 6 Online will be included in the price of the base game or sold separately.

How the claim was built

GameGPU says the information came from an analysis of Take-Two Interactive's financial reports for fiscal year 2027, using a tool called Fable 5.2. The analysis found an anomaly in the company's net bookings forecast of 8 billion dollars, which the site attributes to a major online revenue source launching before March 31, 2027, the end of Take-Two's fiscal year.

That is financial inference, not reporting from a source inside Rockstar. WindowsForum, which covered the claim the same day, points out that GameGPU names no Rockstar source, no retailer and no store database. The claim leans entirely on reading a bookings forecast as a hint, and Take-Two's forecast does not say what that revenue is. It could be GTA 6 Online, or it could be something else entirely, such as stronger than expected console sales or another title.

There is also a simpler reading. GamesBeat reported on October 13, citing four anonymous industry sources, that Rockstar plans to release GTA 6 on PC in the first quarter of 2027. March is the last month of that quarter, so a March date fits the existing report without adding anything new. The GameGPU post does not cite GamesBeat, and it does not add a source of its own.

The fake press release

Separately, a document dressed as an official Rockstar press release, claiming GTA 6 Online launches in March 2027 on every platform including PC, was posted on X on November 24 by an account called Lupen, using the handle @0xLupenn, an account created in July 2026. It was not posted by Rockstar Games.

The document gives itself away in several places. Its headline says "Now Available" while its dateline is March 2027, and its first paragraph says the mode is now live, yet it appeared in November 2026, four months before the date it claims to be reporting from. Its closing line admits that Rockstar Games has not announced a date for GTA 6 Online. The poster said in the post itself that he was using Fable, an AI tool, to make it.

TalkEsport published a debunk of the document on November 25, 2026. Rockstar Newswire, the official channel where Rockstar publishes its announcements, has no such announcement: no GTA 6 Online press release, no Online date and no Online price.

Two rumors, not one

The important point is that these are two different claims that happen to point at the same month. The first is the GameGPU financial inference about a PC and Online launch in March 2027. The second is a fabricated press release about GTA 6 Online in March 2027. They are not the same claim, and neither one is an announcement from Rockstar. Coverage that treats the fake document as support for the GameGPU claim, or the GameGPU claim as support for the fake document, is getting it wrong twice.

There is a third, separate thread worth keeping apart from both. Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy reportedly told Bloomberg that GTA 6's multiplayer mode arrives next year. That is a second-hand remark, not a date, and it says nothing about PC.

What is actually confirmed

Rockstar's official GTA 6 page lists the game as available only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, at a base price of 79.99 dollars. There is no PC edition listed, no Windows release window, no PC pricing and no listing on Steam, the Epic Games Store or the Rockstar Games Launcher.

Take-Two's formal paperwork says the same thing. In its fiscal second-quarter 2027 earnings release, filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on November 9, 2026, the company's future lineup table lists Grand Theft Auto VI solely for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The omission stands out because other games in the same filing do carry PC tags: NBA 2K27 lists PC, and Ghost Story Games' Judas lists PC as a target platform despite not having a release date. When Variety pressed Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick about other platforms, he said the information always comes from Take-Two's labels, meaning Rockstar.

What to watch next

The next official checkpoint is Take-Two's third-quarter earnings call, expected around early February 2027, when the company will have a full quarter of GTA 6 console sales to report. A PC announcement, if one comes, will appear on the Rockstar Newswire first. Until then, any exact PC date you see online is either a report, like GamesBeat's Q1 2027 window, or a guess dressed up as one.
