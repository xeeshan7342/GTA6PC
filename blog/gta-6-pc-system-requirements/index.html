GTA 6 PC system requirements: what we know

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

Everyone wants to know if their PC can run GTA 6. Rockstar hasn't said yet, but the console versions give us a solid starting point.

Short answerThere are no official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. The game was built for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so expect to need a modern 8-core CPU, a current-gen graphics card, 16 GB of RAM or more and well over 200 GB on an SSD. Treat any "official spec list" you see right now as fake.

Why there's no official list yet

Rockstar publishes PC requirements when it announces a PC version, and it hasn't announced one for GTA 6. A GamesBeat report says a PC release is planned for early 2027, so requirements could appear in the weeks before that.

What the console hardware tells us

GTA 6 is a current-gen-only game. It skipped PS4 and Xbox One completely, so it's built around the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Those consoles use:

  • An 8-core AMD Zen 2 processor
  • An AMD RDNA 2 graphics chip, roughly 10 teraflops on PS5 and 12 on Series X
  • 16 GB of shared memory
  • A fast NVMe SSD, which the game relies on to stream its world

PC versions usually need a bit more than the console to match it, because Windows and PC drivers add overhead, and because PC players want higher frame rates and resolutions.

Our estimated specs

These are our estimates based on the console hardware and Rockstar's past PC releases. They are not official.

Storage is the safest prediction

On consoles, GTA 6 is reported to be around a 210 GB download. PC versions are often a little larger once high-resolution textures are included, so plan for well over 200 GB. A hard drive will very likely be too slow, since the game was designed for console SSDs. More in our GTA 6 file size guide.

Should you upgrade now?

Not yet. Prices change, and the real requirements could be higher or lower than anyone expects. If you're building a PC anyway, an 8-core CPU, 32 GB of RAM, a 1 TB or 2 TB NVMe SSD and a current graphics card is a safe target for GTA 6 and other big games.

Beware fake "official" spec lists

Spec sheets claiming to be official GTA 6 PC requirements have been circulating for months. Rockstar hasn't released any. The only place official requirements will appear is Rockstar's own site and the store pages once the PC version is announced.
