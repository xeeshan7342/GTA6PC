Will GTA 6 run on a gaming laptop?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

A lot of players game on laptops, especially students and people who move around. GTA 6's PC requirements aren't out yet, but here's what's likely to work.

Short answerA recent gaming laptop with an RTX 4060/5060 or better, 16 GB of RAM (32 GB ideal) and a fast SSD with 250 GB free should be a reasonable target for GTA 6 at 1080p, likely with upscaling. Thin office laptops with integrated graphics almost certainly won't cut it. These are estimates.

Laptop GPUs aren't desktop GPUs

A laptop "RTX 4070" is usually noticeably slower than the desktop card with the same name, because laptops have tighter power and cooling limits. Two laptops with the same GPU can also perform very differently depending on how much power the maker allows it to use. Check reviews that show the GPU's wattage and real game benchmarks.

Cooling and power matter

  • Play plugged in. Laptops cut performance heavily on battery.
  • Use the maker's performance mode and keep the vents clear.
  • A cooling pad can help keep clocks stable in long sessions.

Storage is easy to overlook

GTA 6 is reported at around 210 GB on consoles, and the PC version will likely be bigger. Many laptops ship with a 512 GB SSD, which fills up fast. Check whether yours has a second M.2 slot for an extra drive. More in our file size guide.

What won't work well

Thin office laptops and ultrabooks with integrated graphics, and older gaming laptops with GTX-series GPUs, are very unlikely to run GTA 6 well. If that's what you have, Remote Play from a console is a better option.

Should you buy a laptop now?

Only if you need one anyway. Official requirements should arrive before the PC version, which is reported for early 2027, and laptop prices often drop around new GPU launches.
