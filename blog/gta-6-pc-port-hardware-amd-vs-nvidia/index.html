What PC do the GTA 6 port builds run on? AMD vs NVIDIA so far

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 27, 2026

Every GTA 6 PC build clip comes with the same question in the comments: what PC is that running on? The developers have shared more than you might think. Here is every spec and frame rate reported so far, and what you can and can't take from them.

Short answerThe fastest verified unofficial GTA 6 PC build, AvatarManz's, averages about 16 fps on an AMD RX 7900 XTX with a Core i9-12900K and 64 GB of RAM. Most builds run at 1 to 10 fps, and the leading Belobug build needs about 22 GB of RAM and more than 16 GB of VRAM. AMD builds lead because the leaked game was made for the Xbox's AMD graphics. None of this reflects what the official PC version will need.

Every reported spec so far

These figures come from PortWatch's tracker and from the projects' own notes, as of November 27, 2026. Frame rates vary a lot by scene, so treat each one as a rough guide.

The headline is simple: even on top-end PCs, none of these builds is close to a playable frame rate in the open world yet.

Why AMD builds are ahead

GTA 6 was made for the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the leaked build is the Xbox version. Both consoles use AMD graphics, so the game's shaders and rendering are written for AMD's architecture. A compatibility layer running on a Radeon card has less to translate than one running on a GeForce card.

The results reflect that. Belobug's AMD-tuned build took the top spot on PortWatch's board at 77.4%, with the best rendering score PortWatch has given so far. The previous leader, ZETTROX's NVIDIA-tuned build, scored 64.2% and drew anything in shadow as black. NVIDIA builds aren't far behind on frame rate, though: Kelvaroq and darkworldhm both report around 10 to 15 fps.

The real bottleneck is memory

Frame rate gets the attention, but memory is what stops most people from running these builds. Belobug's build needs about 22 GB of system RAM and more VRAM than the 16 GB on its RX 9070 XT. AvatarManz's build uses 40 to 46 GB of memory. Most testers are on 64 GB machines.

That most likely comes from how the builds work. A console shares one pool of fast memory between CPU and GPU, and the compatibility layers emulate that on PC with extra copies and translation along the way. It is one of the clearest signs that these are experiments, not ports.

What this tells you about the real PC version

Very little. An official PC version would be compiled for Windows and PC graphics APIs, with no translation layer in between, so it would run far better on the same hardware. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and the leaked builds can't stand in for them.

What the builds do show is that GTA 6 is a heavy game, and that 16 GB of VRAM and plenty of system RAM are a sensible baseline for anyone planning a new PC. For realistic guidance, see our expected GTA 6 PC system requirements and best GPU for GTA 6. Our advice hasn't changed: don't buy hardware for GTA 6 until Rockstar publishes the real numbers.

Can you run one of these builds?

We don't recommend it. Every build needs the leaked Xbox game files, several projects have already been taken down, and "ready to play" packages are a common malware trap. If you want to play GTA 6 on your PC screen today, PS Remote Play from a PS5 is the safe route.
