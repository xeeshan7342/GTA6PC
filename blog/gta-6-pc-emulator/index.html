GTA 6 PC emulator: the honest status

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

PS5-on-PC projects are moving fast, and that makes the emulator headlines believable. Here is what those projects have really achieved, and the one missing piece that stops GTA 6 before it starts.

Short answerNo emulator can run GTA 6 today. The game has not been dumped from a PS5, because it needs firmware 14.23 and public jailbreaks stop at 13.60. Without a copy of the game, no emulator or native conversion project can even start testing it.

The blocker: GTA 6 has not been dumped

Every emulator and every native conversion project needs the same first ingredient: a copy of the game taken from a console, called a dump. GTA 6 requires PS5 firmware 14.23, released just before launch. Public PS5 exploits stop at firmware 13.60, so there is no public way to copy GTA 6 off a PS5. A researcher has reported dumping an unnamed game on firmware 14.22, one version older, with no proof published and no method shared.

Until a dump exists, emulator progress on GTA 6 is exactly zero, whatever a video title says. The live AnyPS5 tracker says the same thing in plain text at the top of its screen: GTA VI, not dumped.

Why 'it runs GTA V' does not mean GTA 6 is close

GTA V's PS5 version running on PC is a genuine milestone, because it uses the same RAGE engine family. But GTA 6 is a far larger game, needs a newer firmware, and has not been dumped. Each of those is its own problem, and all three have to be solved before the first frame.

How to judge the next emulator claim

  • Does it name the game dump it used, and how it was obtained?
  • Is the result on the project's own compatibility list or GitHub, or only in a video?
  • Can anyone else reproduce it?
  • Is the 'download' hosted somewhere that also asks for your passwords or wallet? Fake emulator repositories have been used to spread exactly that malware.

Quick answers

Is there a GTA 6 emulator that works?

No. No PS5 or Xbox Series emulator can run GTA 6, and the game has not been dumped from a console yet, so there is nothing for an emulator to load.

What is AnyPS5?

AnyPS5 is a community project that converts PS5 games to run natively on Windows and Linux, with no emulator. It rebuilds PS5 system functions one by one and has 161 reported games reaching gameplay, but GTA 6 is not one of them.

What can KytyPS5 run?

KytyPS5 has shown real progress on other titles, including the PS5 version of GTA V running on PC. That is GTA V, not GTA 6, and performance is experimental.
