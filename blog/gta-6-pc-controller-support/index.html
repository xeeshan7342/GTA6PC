Can you play GTA 6 on PC with a controller?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

If you are waiting for GTA 6 on PC, you are probably also wondering how you will play it: controller in hand, or mouse and keyboard on the desk. Rockstar has said nothing about either, but its own PC games give a clear picture of what to expect.

Short answerRockstar has not announced GTA 6 on PC, so controller support is unconfirmed. The pattern points one way: GTA 5 on PC has plug-and-play controller support, and the GTA 5 Enhanced upgrade added DualSense support with adaptive triggers in March 2025. Expect GTA 6 on PC to follow, with full mouse and keyboard controls alongside.

The short answer: unconfirmed, but the pattern is clear

Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6, so it has announced nothing about controllers either. No DualSense support, no Xbox controller support, no keyboard and mouse details. But the question is not a coin flip. Rockstar's PC games have supported controllers for a decade, and its most recent PC release, GTA 5 Enhanced, added DualSense adaptive triggers on PC. Expect GTA 6 on PC to follow the same pattern, while treating every detail as unconfirmed until Rockstar says so.

What GTA 5 Enhanced did on PC

The best evidence is Rockstar's own most recent work. On February 20, 2025, Rockstar announced a free upgrade for GTA 5 on PC, called GTA 5 Enhanced, released March 4, 2025. The upgrade brought PC players the features previously exclusive to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, including ray-traced ambient occlusion and global illumination, AMD FSR and NVIDIA DLSS upscaling, faster loading with DirectStorage, and DualSense controller support with adaptive triggers.

Rockstar described the DualSense support in its announcement: haptic feedback and dynamic resistance through the adaptive triggers, for effects including weather, rough road surfaces, explosions and directional damage. That is a console controller feature, built for the PS5 pad, running on a Windows PC. It is the clearest sign of where Rockstar's controller support stands today.

Before Enhanced, GTA 5 on PC already had plug-and-play controller support. Rockstar's original PC release announcement described extensive configurable mouse and keyboard controls alongside controller support, and the game has supported Xbox and PlayStation controllers on PC since 2015.

Xbox controllers: the easy case

If you play with an Xbox controller, expect the simplest experience. Windows is built by Microsoft, Xbox controllers are native to Windows, and GTA 5 on PC picks them up with no setup. Wired over USB or wireless over Bluetooth or the Xbox wireless adapter, the pad works, and the game switches between controller and keyboard input on the fly.

That is almost certain to carry over to GTA 6. The console versions already run on Xbox controllers, and the PC version will be built from the same game. There is no technical reason for Rockstar to drop support that already works.

DualSense: adaptive triggers should carry over

The DualSense is the more interesting case. On PS5, GTA 6 runs on the DualSense, and on PC, GTA 5 Enhanced already supports the same pad with adaptive triggers. The technology is proven in Rockstar's own PC code, so the likely outcome is that GTA 6 on PC supports the DualSense the same way, with trigger resistance for driving, shooting and weather effects.

There are two practical notes. First, the adaptive trigger effects in GTA 5 Enhanced work over a wired USB connection. Wireless Bluetooth may not carry the same features, so plan on a cable if you want the full effect. Second, Steam can translate DualSense input through Steam Input, which is useful if you buy the game on Steam, but the Rockstar Games Launcher version relies on the game's own support. Our GTA 6 on Steam page covers the store question.

Keyboard and mouse: the PC advantage

Keyboard and mouse is where PC has always had the edge in GTA. GTA 5 on PC offers full key rebinding, raw mouse input, sensitivity adjustments and separate sensitivity settings for first and third person. Controllers get adjustable dead zones and several aim assist options instead: Assisted Aim Full, Assisted Aim Partial and Free Aim, with presets rather than full button remapping.

The game also supports dynamic input switching. You can drive with a controller, then put it down and pick up the mouse for a shootout, without changing settings or restarting. That is the setup most PC players will probably use in GTA 6: controller for driving and flying, mouse and keyboard for on-foot combat. Our PC build guide covers the hardware side.

What about aim assist and GTA 6 Online?

Aim assist is the one controller question that could matter for fairness. In GTA 5, controller players get aim assist options that mouse players do not need, and GTA Online on PC has kept controller and mouse players in the same sessions. Rockstar has announced no crossplay for GTA 6, and no online mode at all yet, so there is no console lobby for PC controller players to join. Our crossplay page and GTA 6 Online page track both questions.

Until Rockstar announces the PC version, none of this is confirmed. But if you already own a controller, keep it. You will almost certainly be able to use it, and the DualSense is the pad most likely to get the full treatment.

Quick answers

Can you play GTA 6 on PC with a controller?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has announced no PC version and no controller details. But GTA 5 on PC supports Xbox and PlayStation controllers, so GTA 6 almost certainly will too.

Will GTA 6 on PC support DualSense adaptive triggers?

Probably, but not confirmed. GTA 5 Enhanced on PC added DualSense support with adaptive triggers in March 2025, and that is the best guide to what GTA 6 will do.

Is keyboard and mouse better than a controller for GTA 6 on PC?

For shooting, yes. A mouse aims faster and more precisely. For driving and flying, most players prefer a controller. GTA 5 on PC lets you switch between the two mid-session.

Do I need a controller to play GTA 6 on PC?

No. GTA 5 on PC plays fully with mouse and keyboard, including full key rebinding. A controller is optional.

Will GTA 6 on PC have aim assist?

Unknown. GTA 5 on PC offers three aim assist settings for controller players. Nothing has been announced for GTA 6.
