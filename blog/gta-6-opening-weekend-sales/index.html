How many copies did GTA 6 sell in its opening weekend?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and the sales numbers coming out of its first weekend are the biggest in entertainment history. The official figure covers day one. The weekend total is an estimate from a named analyst firm, and the two should not be mixed up.

Short answerAnalyst firm Alinea Analytics estimates GTA 6 sold 44.9 million copies and grossed more than $4 billion in its first four days. The official figure from Take-Two is $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales from 32.5 million pre-orders. The weekend total is an estimate, not an official announcement.

The official numbers: $3.79 billion on day one

Take-Two Interactive announced the official day-one figures on November 20, 2026. Grand Theft Auto VI sold 32.5 million units before release, a record across all platforms, and day-one retail sales reached $3.79 billion. Take-Two called it the biggest entertainment launch in history, and the game holds a 99 score on Metacritic. Those are the only official sales figures so far: pre-orders plus launch day, across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The $3.79 billion figure covers retail sales, which means copies bought by players, not copies shipped to stores. It does not include any PC sales, because there is no PC version, and it does not include GTA Online revenue, because the online mode has not launched yet. Our sales forecast page has the pre-launch predictions it beat.

The weekend estimate: 44.9 million copies, more than $4 billion

The first weekend numbers come from Alinea Analytics, a games market research firm. Its head of market analysis, Rhys Elliott, said on November 23 that GTA 6 sold an estimated 44.9 million copies through its first weekend, covering sales through Sunday, November 22. The same estimate puts gross revenue at more than $4 billion in four days.

The split is heavily weighted to PlayStation. Alinea estimates 33.7 million copies on PS5, or 75.1 percent of the total, and 11.2 million on Xbox Series X|S, or 24.9 percent. That means about 12.4 million copies sold after launch day, on top of the 32.5 million pre-orders Take-Two confirmed. Sensor Tower gave a similar picture for day one, estimating that 75 percent of its 24.58 million day-one digital sales were on PlayStation.

Alinea also estimates that around 40.8 million people played GTA 6 on Sunday, November 22, with about 76 percent of them on PS5, and that roughly 9 million players had already spent more than 20 hours in the game. Those are estimates, not official figures, and Alinea has not published its method or margin of error for physical sales. Take-Two and Rockstar have not announced official opening weekend figures.

How big is $4 billion?

For scale, GTA 5 made about $1 billion in its first three days in 2013 and about $1.45 billion across its first three months. GTA 6 passed that in a single day. GameSpot noted that $4 billion in four days is roughly equal to the combined box office of 2026's two biggest films, Spider-Man: Brand New Day at $2.5 billion and The Odyssey at $1.8 billion. Polygon put it another way: in four days GTA 6 made about twice as much as Taylor Swift's entire Eras Tour.

The franchise total moved too. Take-Two's confirmed figures put the Grand Theft Auto series past $14.4 billion in revenue and over 512 million units sold, and Alinea's estimates push that past 520 million units. GTA 6 alone is already one of the best-selling games of all time after four days, and it did it on two platforms, without PC and without an online mode.

What this means for PC players

Every copy in these figures was sold on a console. PC players are not in the numbers yet, because Rockstar has not announced a PC version. That is the point that matters most for the next sales wave. A GamesBeat report from October says Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027 for the PC release, though Rockstar has not confirmed it. Our release date page tracks that story.

When the PC version does arrive, it will be a second launch. GTA 5 sold tens of millions more copies on PC after its console debut, and Rockstar's PC versions have historically added a large second wave. Take-Two's next earnings call is the next likely moment for official updated figures, and it is also a likely moment for PC news. Until then, treat the $4 billion figure as a strong estimate from a named analyst firm, and the $3.79 billion as the confirmed record.

Quick answers

How many copies did GTA 6 sell in its opening weekend?

An estimated 44.9 million copies through November 22, according to Alinea Analytics. Take-Two has only officially confirmed 32.5 million pre-orders and $3.79 billion in day-one sales.

How much money did GTA 6 make in its first weekend?

More than $4 billion in gross revenue in four days, according to Alinea Analytics. The official figure is $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales from Take-Two.

Did GTA 6 sell more on PS5 or Xbox?

PS5, by a wide margin. Alinea estimates 33.7 million copies on PS5 (75.1 percent) and 11.2 million on Xbox Series X|S (24.9 percent).

Are the 44.9 million copies official?

No. They are an estimate from Alinea Analytics. Take-Two and Rockstar have not announced official opening weekend figures.

How many copies will GTA 6 sell on PC?

Unknown. There is no PC version yet, so PC sales are zero so far. A PC release is reported for the first quarter of 2027, and it would start a second wave of sales.
