GTA 6 sold 53 million copies in its first week, Alinea estimates

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 28, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI sold an estimated 53 million copies in its first week, worth about $4.7 billion, according to Alinea Analytics. The estimate, published on November 27, 2026, covers roughly eight days since the November 19 launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. Take-Two has not confirmed the figures, and they remain an analyst estimate, not an official publisher total, but every tracker published since launch points the same way.

Short answerAlinea Analytics estimates GTA 6 sold 53 million copies in its first week, generating about $4.7 billion in gross revenue. About 75.7 percent of copies sold on PlayStation 5, roughly 40 million, against about 13 million on Xbox. The figure is an estimate: Take-Two has published no one week total, and its only official figures are $3.79 billion in day one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before release. All of it happened with no PC version.

The headline number

Alinea Analytics estimates that Grand Theft Auto VI sold 53 million copies in its first week on sale, generating about $4.7 billion in gross revenue. Rhys Elliott, the firm's head of market analysis, posted the figures on X on November 27, 2026, and published a longer breakdown on the firm's Substack the same day. GamesBeat and Polygon both reported the estimate within hours. Take-Two has not confirmed the figures, and they remain an analyst estimate, not an official publisher total.

That is 8.1 million copies more than the same firm's launch weekend estimate of 44.9 million copies, published on November 23. The game launched on November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S, so the new estimate covers roughly eight days of sales, and it puts GTA 6 on pace to become one of the best selling games of all time in its first month.

PlayStation did most of the work

About 75.7 percent of the estimated copies sold on PlayStation 5, roughly 40 million, against about 13 million on Xbox Series X and S. The revenue split leans even further toward Sony's console, with 76.1 percent of the estimated revenue coming from PS5, which Alinea attributes to regional prices. The United States alone accounts for 33.5 percent of the PS5 copies sold.

The Xbox share has been shrinking through the week. It was 25.8 percent on November 21 and 25.2 percent on November 22, before settling at 24.3 percent. That pattern fits a launch where PlayStation has the marketing deal and the larger installed base, and where the early rush was strongest on Sony's platform.

Playtime is extreme too. Alinea estimates that 7 million players have already played for more than 50 hours, and about 250,000 have played for more than 100 hours, which is more than 11 hours a day, every day since launch, for the most dedicated group. Sony said separately that more than 39 million unique players played GTA 6 on PS5 in its first four days, the biggest launch in PlayStation history.

How the estimate compares with earlier counts

Every count published since launch points the same way, but they do not count the same thing, so the numbers need care. Take-Two's own figures, announced on November 20, are the only official ones: $3.79 billion in day one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before release. That day one figure is a company estimate of retail sales through the end of launch day.

Sensor Tower, a second tracker, counted 47.8 million copies sold by the end of the launch weekend on Sunday, November 22. Alinea's 44.9 million weekend estimate and its new 53 million week one estimate are sell-through estimates, built from the firm's own market data, not shipments to stores. The gap between Sensor Tower's 47.8 million and Alinea's 44.9 million for the same weekend shows why these are estimates. The direction is not in doubt. Every tracker has GTA 6 past 40 million copies inside four days, and past 50 million inside a week.

The regional picture

Physical sales tell the same story in each big market. In Japan, Famitsu's retail chart puts GTA 6 at 200,759 physical PS5 copies in its opening week, November 16 to 22, enough to take the top spot and to push the PS5 Digital Edition past the Switch 2 as the week's best selling console. The figure covers boxed copies only, and the Japanese retail edition is a code in a box, so it counts those boxed codes.

In the UK, The Game Business estimates 32 million pounds in physical sales over the first three days, the 17th biggest physical launch in the UK since records began in 1995. In France, the game sold more than 1 million physical copies, comparable to GTA V's 2013 launch there, and in Spain it was the largest physical launch on record. None of these figures includes digital sales, which are the majority of the total.

Bigger than GTA V's first six weeks

The comparison with GTA V shows the scale of the jump. In its fiscal second quarter results in October 2013, Take-Two said GTA V had sold in nearly 29 million units in less than six weeks, and had grossed over $800 million in its first 24 hours and over $1 billion in three days. GTA 6 has nearly doubled that six week shipment figure in about one week, and its day one revenue is nearly five times GTA V's.

It is already moving up the all time list. DualShockers reports that the first week total has passed Pokemon Red and Blue on the Game Boy, which sold about 31 million copies across their lifetime. GTA 6 did that in eight days.

What it means for PC players

All of this happened without a PC version. Rockstar has announced no PC version and no PC date, and the GamesBeat report pointing to the first quarter of 2027 remains unconfirmed. Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick has said that on a big title PC can account for 45 to 50 percent of sales, which suggests the PC version, whenever it arrives, could add tens of millions more copies to a total that is already a record.

For now, the sales story is a console story. The PC version is still a rumour, the fan ports are still unofficial, and the only way to play GTA 6 is on a PlayStation 5 or an Xbox Series X or S. Our PC release date status page has the full official record, and our opening weekend sales report has the earlier figures this estimate builds on.

What is still unconfirmed

The 53 million figure is an estimate. Take-Two has published no one week sales figure, and the next official numbers are likely to come with the company's next earnings release. Alinea's platform split, revenue total and playtime counts are estimates too, and Elliott has said he will publish a longer analysis on Substack. Until Take-Two speaks, treat the exact totals as moving estimates, and the record itself as settled: GTA 6 is the biggest entertainment launch ever by sales, and it did it in a week, on two consoles, with no PC version at all.
