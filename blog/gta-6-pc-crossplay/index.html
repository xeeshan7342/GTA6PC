Will GTA 6 on PC have crossplay?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

If your friends play on PS5 or Xbox and you are waiting for the PC version, one question matters more than the release date: will you be able to play together? Right now the answer is no, and Rockstar has not announced any change.

Short answerNo crossplay has been announced for GTA 6. The game launches as a single-player experience with no online mode, and GTA Online has never supported play between PlayStation, Xbox and PC. Until Rockstar says otherwise, assume PC players will be separate from console players.

The short answer

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game. Rockstar has described it in those exact terms, calling Grand Theft Auto VI a single-player experience, and it has announced no online mode and no crossplay for any platform. That means there is currently no version of GTA 6 where a PC player and a console player share a session, because there is no PC version and no multiplayer mode to share.

The question still matters, because the PC version is widely expected. A GamesBeat report from October 13, 2026 says Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027 for PC, though Rockstar has not confirmed it. When that version arrives, PC players will want to know whether they can play with friends on PS5 and Xbox. Based on everything Rockstar has done before, the honest answer is probably not, unless Rockstar announces a change. Our GTA 6 PC release date page tracks the PC launch story.

What GTA Online does today

The best guide to what Rockstar will do is what it already does. GTA Online has never supported crossplay between PlayStation, Xbox and PC. Rockstar Support states it plainly: players on PC cannot play with players on console. Each platform family runs its own separate GTA Online, with its own sessions, crews and economy.

There is one limited exception, and it only applies inside PC. Players who bought GTA V through different PC stores, Steam, the Epic Games Store, the Rockstar Games Launcher or the Xbox app on PC, can play together through cross-network play, as long as everyone is running the same PC edition. The Xbox app on a PC does not connect you to Xbox console players. And the two PC editions cannot play together either: Enhanced and Legacy run separate online instances.

Console generations are separate too. A PS5 player running the native PS5 version cannot join PS4 players, and the same applies on Xbox. The workaround is to run the older console version on the newer console through backward compatibility, which is not the same as cross-generation matchmaking.

Crossplay is not the same as cross-progression

Two different ideas get mixed up here, and the difference matters. Crossplay means playing together at the same time. Cross-progression means your character and progress follow you between platforms. GTA Online has neither, but it has offered something in between: a one-time profile migration.

When GTA Online moved to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in March 2022, Rockstar let players move an eligible profile from PS4 or Xbox One to the new console, and from PC Legacy to PC Enhanced. That move happens once. After it, the original profile stays where it was, and progress made on one version does not sync to the other. Purchased GTA$ only transfers within the same console family, and progress cannot move between a console and PC at all.

A Rockstar Social Club account sits above all of this. One account can be linked to PlayStation, Xbox and PC, and your friends list, stats and profile follow the account. Your character, money, properties and vehicles do not. They stay tied to the platform where they were earned. So linking accounts does not create crossplay, and it will not do so for GTA 6 unless Rockstar changes the system.

Why Rockstar has never added it

Rockstar has never given a full public explanation for the lack of crossplay in GTA Online. The reasons most often cited by people who follow the game are practical ones. PC has a different anti-cheat situation, with modding far more widespread than on consoles. Each platform has its own economy and player-made content. And merging years of character data across platforms that were never designed to share it would be a large engineering job.

None of that is an official Rockstar statement, so treat it as analysis, not fact. What is factual is the outcome: after more than a decade of GTA Online, across three console generations and two PC editions, Rockstar has never switched crossplay on. That is the record any prediction about GTA 6 has to argue against.

What could change for GTA 6

There are reasons to think the next online mode could be different. Most big online games now ship with crossplay, and players expect it. If Rockstar builds GTA 6 Online from scratch rather than carrying GTA Online forward, it could design crossplay in from the start. But none of that has been announced, and Rockstar has not even announced an online mode for GTA 6 yet.

The pattern from Rockstar's last two launches is that online follows the story mode. GTA Online arrived about two weeks after GTA V in 2013, and Red Dead Online arrived about a month after Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018. If GTA 6 follows the same pattern, an online mode would come after launch, and any crossplay decision would be announced with it, not before. Our GTA 6 Online on PC guide follows that story.

One more thing to keep in mind: the PC version itself is still unannounced. Crossplay between PC and console cannot exist before the PC version does. The reported Q1 2027 window for PC is the earliest point the question becomes real, and it is still only a report.

What this means for you

If your friends play GTA 6 on PS5 or Xbox and you are waiting for PC, plan as if you will not be able to play together. That is the current position, and nothing Rockstar has said points the other way. If playing together matters more than playing on PC, the only certain route is to buy the console version. Our wait for PC or play on console now guide works through that decision.

If you are set on PC, the thing to watch for is not a rumour or a leak. It is an announcement from Rockstar itself, either that GTA 6 Online is coming or that crossplay is supported. We will update this page the day either one lands.

Quick answers

Will GTA 6 have crossplay on PC?

No crossplay has been announced. GTA 6 launches as a single-player game with no online mode, and Rockstar has said nothing about cross-platform play for a future online mode.

Does GTA Online have crossplay?

No. GTA Online does not support crossplay between PlayStation, Xbox and PC. Rockstar Support states that players on PC cannot play with players on console.

Can PC players play GTA Online with console friends?

No. Each platform family has its own separate GTA Online. PC players can only play with other PC players using the same edition, Enhanced or Legacy.

Will my GTA Online character transfer to PC?

Rockstar has offered one-time profile migrations between console generations and from PC Legacy to PC Enhanced, but progress does not keep syncing afterwards, and you cannot transfer between PlayStation and Xbox. Nothing has been announced for GTA 6.

Could GTA 6 Online add crossplay later?

It is possible but not guaranteed. Most modern online games offer crossplay, but Rockstar's record with GTA Online points the other way, and no announcement exists.
