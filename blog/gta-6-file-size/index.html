How big is GTA 6? File size on PS5, Xbox and PC

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

GTA 6 is one of the biggest games ever released. Here's how much space it takes, and how much to clear for the PC version.

Short answerGTA 6 is reported at about 210 GB on PS5, and the leaked Xbox build was reported at about 209 GB. For PC, plan for 230–250 GB free on an SSD. Rockstar hasn't published a PC size yet.

GTA 6 size on PS5

Before launch, the PlayStation Game Size tracker reported GTA 6 as roughly a 210 GB download on PS5. Other reports put the space needed at around 212 GB. Day-one patches can add more on top.

GTA 6 size on Xbox

There's no widely reported official Xbox figure. The leaked Xbox build was reported at about 209 GB, which is in line with PS5. Series S owners should note that the 512 GB console only has about 364 GB free for games, so GTA 6 takes up more than half of it.

How big will GTA 6 be on PC?

Rockstar hasn't said. PC versions are often a bit larger than console versions because they ship higher-resolution textures and don't use the consoles' hardware decompression in the same way. Red Dead Redemption 2 needed 150 GB on PC, more than its console install.

Our estimate: plan for 230–250 GB of free space, and more if you want room for patches.

Do you need an SSD?

Very likely, yes. GTA 6 was designed around the fast SSDs in PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, which stream the open world as you move. On a hard drive, expect long loads and pop-in, if it's supported at all. An NVMe SSD is the safest choice.

How to get ready

  • Check your free space now, and clear old games you don't play.
  • If you're buying a new drive, a 1 TB or 2 TB NVMe SSD gives room for GTA 6 and future patches.
  • Don't download anything claiming to be the "GTA 6 PC files." One fake 113 GB "PC ISO" turned out to be malware. More in our scam guide.

Last updated November 24, 2026. We update this page when Rockstar announces anything new.
