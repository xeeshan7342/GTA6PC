GTA 6 PC launch: what to expect

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Rockstar hasn't announced the PC version yet, but its last two PC launches give a decent idea of how this one will go. Here's what to expect, and what's still unknown.

Short answerGTA 6 has no announced PC launch yet. Based on GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, expect it on the Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam and Epic, with PC extras like unlocked frame rates and ultrawide support. Official system requirements haven't been published.

Where it will probably launch

Rockstar's recent PC releases have shipped on its own Rockstar Games Launcher, with Steam and the Epic Games Store alongside it. Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on the Rockstar Games Launcher and Epic in November 2019, and arrived on Steam about a month later. It's reasonable to expect GTA 6 to follow a similar path, though Rockstar hasn't said so.

PC features players can expect

Rockstar's PC versions have usually been more than straight copies of the console game. GTA V and RDR2 on PC both added:

  • Unlocked frame rates and support for high refresh rate monitors
  • Ultrawide and multi-monitor support
  • A much longer list of graphics settings
  • Full mouse and keyboard controls
  • Higher-resolution textures and longer draw distances

None of this is confirmed for GTA 6 yet, but it's the pattern.

What about mods and roleplay?

PC is where GTA modding and roleplay servers live. In 2023, Rockstar bought Cfx.re, the team behind the FiveM and RedM roleplay platforms. That's a strong sign Rockstar wants community servers to be part of GTA's future on PC, but it hasn't said anything specific about GTA 6.

System requirements

Rockstar hasn't published PC system requirements for GTA 6. Lists claiming to show the official minimum or recommended specs are made up, so don't buy hardware based on them.

What's safe to say: GTA 6 is a current-gen-only game, so expect a modern 6- or 8-core CPU, a recent graphics card, 16 GB of RAM or more, and an SSD. Storage will be large. The leaked Xbox build was reported at around 209 GB, so plan for well over 200 GB of free space.

Price and editions

PC pricing and editions haven't been announced. Console editions are already on sale, and PC versions of Rockstar games have usually launched at a similar price to consoles.

GTA 6 Online on PC

Rockstar hasn't set a date for GTA 6's online mode on any platform yet, so there's no PC date for it either.

How to get the news first

Keep an eye on the Rockstar Newswire. We'll update our GTA 6 PC release date page as soon as anything official is announced.
