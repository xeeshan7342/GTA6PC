Is GTA 6 on Mac?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 25, 2026

GTA 6 is out on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Mac owners are asking the same question PC players are asking, with one extra problem: Rockstar has never released a mainline GTA game for macOS. There is no Mac version today, no Mac release date, and no official download. There are viral clips claiming otherwise, a real but very early community project, and one legitimate way to play right now.

Short answerThere is no GTA 6 version for Mac and Rockstar has not announced one. The viral Mac gameplay clips are unverified. The only legitimate way to play on a Mac today is Remote Play from your own PS5 or Xbox. A future official PC version could eventually reach Macs through compatibility tools like CrossOver, but that path does not exist yet.

The short answer: no Mac version

MacRumors reported on launch day, November 19, 2026, that GTA 6 is not available on Mac or Apple TV. The game is limited to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. It is not on macOS or tvOS, or any other platform, including Windows and Nintendo Switch.

That is not a surprise given Rockstar's history. Rockstar never released an official, native version of GTA 5 for macOS, and the same is true of Red Dead Redemption 2. French outlet MacGeneration, writing before launch, called a native Mac version "highly unlikely" for exactly that reason, noting that Rockstar's few Apple ports are old iOS titles like Bully.

There is also no announced Mac date to wait for. Rockstar has not announced a PC version either, and a Mac version would come after a PC version, if it comes at all. Our PC release date status page tracks the reported Q1 2027 window and what Rockstar and Take-Two have actually said.

The viral Mac clips, checked

Since November 24, clips have circulated on Threads and Instagram claiming to show GTA 6 running on macOS. One Threads post from November 25 says the build is progressing fast and estimates smooth 60 frames per second gameplay within about two weeks. An Instagram reel from November 24 shows a laptop screen with in-game scenes and the headline "SOMEONE IS PORTING GTA 6 TO MAC". A separate report from November 20 claims GTA 6 was ported to run natively on macOS on ARM64, crediting OpenAI's Codex.

None of these claims has been verified by an independent outlet. The footage itself looks unfinished, with flat lighting, stiff animation and distorted faces, which fits an early build or AI-upscaled renders as much as it fits a real port. The Free Press Journal, reporting on the Take-Two crackdown on November 25, says only that one project has "a heavily degraded version running on Mac", with no detail and no verification.

The strongest grounded evidence comes from the live port tracker, which reads the public project repositories every 15 minutes. Its November 25 update says the XSXEmu project now runs the game's own code under Rosetta on Apple Silicon, starts its worker threads and opens its archives, but has not created a graphics device, run a shader or produced a frame, and says no game is playable. In other words, the furthest verified Mac result is code starting, not a game running. Until a reputable source confirms footage, or the tracker records a Mac build producing frames, the viral clips stay in the unverified column.

The only legitimate way to play on a Mac today

If you own a console, you can play GTA 6 on a Mac screen right now through Remote Play. The game runs on your console and streams to your Mac, so your Mac's hardware does not matter much, but you do need the console and the game.

MethodStatus for Mac users
PS5 Remote PlayWorks. Sony provides a Mac app, and Rockstar has confirmed GTA 6 supports PS Remote Play.
Xbox Remote PlayWorks. Microsoft's browser-based Remote Play runs in a supported browser on Mac.

Remote Play is also the only route our Remote Play guide recommends for PC players, and the same logic applies on Mac. It is streaming, not a port, and it is the only method Rockstar itself supports. Our GeForce Now explainer has the full picture on cloud gaming, which is still waiting on a PC version too.

The future route: PC version first, then CrossOver

The realistic path to GTA 6 on a Mac starts with an official Windows version. MacGeneration points to CrossOver, a compatibility layer that already runs GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 on Apple Silicon Macs, along with newer games like Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 and Helldivers 2. Once a Windows version of GTA 6 exists, the community could try the same route, and Apple also ships its own Game Porting Toolkit for running Windows games on Mac.

Former Rockstar technical director Obbe Vermeij told Ars Technica that AI-assisted reverse engineering could change the picture, citing a fan remake that ran on Mac less than 48 hours after release. MacGeneration adds that this is legally dubious and would be harder for GTA 6, and warns that the game's protections, graphics requirements and engine could block compatibility, and that a recent chip would be needed.

So the order of events is fixed: official PC version first, compatibility work second. A Mac version before a PC version is not a realistic expectation, and anyone offering a Mac download today is offering a fake. Our download scams guide explains what those installers actually contain.

What to watch next

Watch for an official PC announcement from Rockstar first, since that is the gate for everything else. Then watch the CrossOver compatibility database and the live port tracker for a Mac entry that produces frames rather than just starting code. If a Mac build ever reaches a real picture, the tracker will show it, and reputable outlets will report it. Until then, every "GTA 6 on Mac" video deserves the same check as every "GTA 6 on PC" video: is there code behind it, has anyone independent confirmed it, and is anyone asking you to download something?

Do not download anything claiming to be GTA 6 for Mac or PC. There is no legitimate download for either platform, and fake installers using the leak as bait are already circulating.

Quick answers

Is GTA 6 available on Mac?

No. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has not announced a macOS version, and there is no Mac download or release date.

Can I play GTA 6 on a Mac?

Yes, but only by streaming it from your own console. PS Remote Play has a Mac app, and Xbox Remote Play works through a browser. The game runs on the console, not on the Mac.

Will GTA 6 come to Mac?

Nothing is confirmed. Rockstar never released GTA 5 or Red Dead Redemption 2 for macOS, so a native Mac version looks unlikely. The realistic route is an official PC version first, then compatibility tools like CrossOver.

Are the viral GTA 6 Mac videos real?

They are unverified. Clips shared on November 24 and 25 claim to show GTA 6 running on macOS, but no independent outlet has confirmed them, and the live port tracker records no Mac build producing a frame. Treat them as unconfirmed until a reputable source verifies them.

Can CrossOver run GTA 6 on Mac?

Not today. CrossOver runs Windows games on Apple Silicon Macs, and it already runs GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, but it needs a Windows version of GTA 6 to work with, and no PC version exists yet.
