Can you pre-order GTA 6 on PC?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Search for a GTA 6 PC pre-order and you will find listings, keys and countdowns. None of them is real. There is no PC version to pre-order, no store page, and no price, and every site taking your money for one today is selling something that does not exist.

Short answerNo, you cannot pre-order GTA 6 on PC. Rockstar has announced no PC version, and there is no listing on Steam, the Epic Games Store or the Rockstar Games Launcher. Console pre-orders are open at $79.99 and $99.99, but no PC key sold anywhere today is real.

The short answer: no store, no listing, no key

As of November 23, 2026, there is no GTA 6 PC pre-order. Not on Steam, not on the Epic Games Store, not on the Rockstar Games Launcher, and not anywhere else. Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6, so there is no PC product for any store to sell. Rockstar's official GTA VI page lists two platforms, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Take-Two's own lineup from its November 9 earnings lists the game for those two platforms only.

That has been checked repeatedly. Reviewers who looked for a PC store page in early November found nothing, and nothing has appeared since the console launch on November 19. There is no PC price, no PC system requirements and no coming-soon listing. If a website is showing you a PC pre-order button, it is not selling the game. See our GTA 6 on Steam page for the suspicious app page that appeared around launch.

What the console pre-order looks like

Console pre-orders are real, and they show what a PC pre-order would probably look like when it eventually opens. Grand Theft Auto VI sells on the Xbox store at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, and PlayStation prices match. Digital pre-orders came with one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's membership for GTA Online, which has led some players to assume GTA 6 itself is part of a subscription. It is not. GTA+ covers GTA Online only.

Console digital pre-load began on November 10, nine days before launch. That matters because it shows the shape of a real Rockstar launch: a dated pre-load, a store listing months in advance, and editions announced on Rockstar's own channels. None of those exist for PC. A real PC pre-order will look like that, and it will come from Rockstar, not from a key reseller.

The fake PC keys and listings

The gap between demand and supply is exactly what scammers use. Listings offering GTA 6 PC keys, early access, or a discounted pre-order have appeared on key reseller sites and in social media ads since before launch. None of them can be real, because there is no PC build to sell a key for. A key for a product that does not exist is a string of characters, not a game.

The risk is not just a wasted purchase. Fake GTA 6 PC downloads have been circulating since the console launch, and security researchers at NordVPN reported that a ready-made GTA 6 PC scam kit was being sold to criminals for about $200. The pattern is familiar: a free download or a cheap key, then malware or a stolen account. We cover the warning signs in our fake GTA 6 PC downloads guide, and we do not link to any of these listings.

A simple test works for any listing you find. If it is not on rockstargames.com, the PlayStation Store, the Xbox store, Steam, Epic or the Rockstar Games Launcher, it is not an official pre-order. And if it claims to sell a PC version today, it fails the test on its face, because Rockstar has not announced one.

When a real PC pre-order could open

A PC pre-order can only open after Rockstar announces the PC version, and Rockstar has a habit of announcing PC versions late. Red Dead Redemption 2 for PC was announced on October 4, 2019, about a month before its November 5 release. The Red Dead Redemption PC port was announced in early October 2024, about three weeks before it came out. A GTA 6 PC announcement could come just as late, and a pre-order window could be short.

The timing question has one reported answer. On October 13, 2026, GamesBeat reported that Rockstar plans to release GTA 6 on PC in the first quarter of 2027, citing four industry sources. Rockstar did not comment. Treat that as a report, not a date, and remember that GTA 6 has already slipped twice on consoles. Our release date page tracks that story in full, including what Rockstar's own history suggests.

One analyst estimate is also in circulation. Michael Pachter has said the PC version could arrive in about 6 or 7 months. That is an analyst's estimate, not a Rockstar statement, and it points to a later window than the GamesBeat report. The honest position is that nobody outside Rockstar knows, and published guesses run from early 2027 to mid-2028.

Where it will probably be sold

Nothing is announced, but Rockstar's recent PC launches give a strong hint. Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on the Rockstar Games Launcher and the Epic Games Store first, and reached Steam about a month later. GTA V and the Red Dead Redemption port followed the same pattern of launching across the Launcher, Steam and Epic. Expect GTA 6 to be sold through those three stores, and expect the Launcher to be part of it from day one.

That also tells you what a real announcement will look like. It will come from Rockstar, as a Newswire post or a PC option on the official GTA VI page, and store pages will appear around the same time. Until then, no countdown, no listing and no key is worth your money.

What to do instead of paying now

Watch the official channels. The Rockstar Newswire is where a PC announcement will land, and Take-Two's next quarterly results in early 2027 are the next obvious moment for news. If you want the prices that do exist, our GTA 6 PC price guide has the console editions and what they mean for PC.

And if you already paid for a PC key or a pre-order from an unofficial seller, treat it as a scam until proven otherwise. Contact your bank or payment provider about a chargeback, change any password you used on that site, and do not download anything it sent you.

Quick answers

Can you pre-order GTA 6 on PC?

No. There is no official PC pre-order on Steam, the Epic Games Store or the Rockstar Games Launcher, because Rockstar has not announced a PC version.

Is there a GTA 6 PC key for sale?

No real one. Any listing selling a GTA 6 PC key is fake, because there is no PC build to sell a key for.

How much will GTA 6 cost on PC?

Unknown. The console Standard Edition is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99, and PC versions of Rockstar games have usually launched near the console price, but Rockstar has announced no PC price.

When will PC pre-orders open?

Only after Rockstar announces the PC version. Rockstar has announced PC versions late before: Red Dead Redemption 2 for PC was announced about a month before release.

Where will GTA 6 on PC be sold?

Not announced. Rockstar's recent PC games launched on the Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam and Epic, with Red Dead Redemption 2 reaching Steam about a month after the other stores.
