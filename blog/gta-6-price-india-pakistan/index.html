GTA 6 price in India and Pakistan

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

GTA 6 is a big purchase anywhere, and especially in South Asia, where console and game prices take a bigger share of income. Here's what it costs in India and Pakistan.

Short answerIn India, GTA 6 is listed at Rs 5,999 (Standard) and Rs 7,499 (Ultimate) on PS5. In Pakistan there's no official rupee price, and estimates based on the $79.99 US price put it around PKR 22,000+. PC pricing isn't announced anywhere.

GTA 6 price in India

EditionPrice
StandardRs 5,999
UltimateRs 7,499

These are the prices reported from PlayStation India's listings. Several outlets say the same prices apply on Xbox, but check the Microsoft Store on your console for the exact rupee price. Older articles predicting Rs 9,000 to Rs 12,000 or more were speculation from before the official listing.

For comparison, the US price is $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, so Indian pricing is noticeably lower in real terms.

GTA 6 price in Pakistan

There's no official Rockstar or PlayStation price in Pakistani rupees. Pakistani outlets have estimated about PKR 22,000 for the Standard Edition and around PKR 28,000 for special editions, based on the dollar price. Physical copies from importers can cost more, and a report of a Rs 64,000 listing came from retailer pages that were later removed and never confirmed.

The console is the bigger cost. Pakistani reports put PS5 prices at roughly Rs 140,000 to Rs 180,000, with GTA 6 hype pushing prices up.

What will GTA 6 cost on PC in India and Pakistan?

Rockstar hasn't announced PC pricing anywhere. Rockstar usually prices PC versions close to consoles, and Steam often uses regional pricing in India. That makes it likely, but not confirmed, that the PC version will cost around the console price in India. See our PC price guide.

For many players in South Asia, the bigger question is hardware. A capable PC can cost more than a console, but it can be upgraded over time and plays thousands of other games. Our PC build guide has budget options.

Avoid cheap "keys" and "PC versions"

There's no GTA 6 PC key for sale anywhere, in any country. Cheap "GTA 6 PC" offers on marketplaces, Facebook pages or WhatsApp groups are scams. Only buy from official stores.
