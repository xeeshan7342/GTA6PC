Will GTA 6 on PC use BattlEye anti-cheat?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

GTA Online on PC was famous for cheaters for almost a decade before Rockstar acted. In September 2024 the studio finally added BattlEye anti-cheat to the PC version. With GTA 6 on PC still unannounced, the obvious question is whether its online mode will ship with the same protection from day one.

Short answerRockstar has not announced any anti-cheat for GTA 6 on PC. What we know is the precedent: GTA Online on PC has used BattlEye since September 17, 2024, and it is mandatory for online play but can be switched off for Story Mode. BattlEye is the likely choice for GTA 6, but that is an expectation, not a confirmed fact.

What Rockstar did for GTA Online

On September 17, 2024, Rockstar released title update 1.69 for the PC version of GTA 5. The headline change was one line in the patch notes: integrated BattlEye Anti-Cheat for GTA Online. It was the first official anti-cheat on GTA Online for PC, nine years after GTA 5 launched on PC, and it arrived on the 11th anniversary of the game's original release.

BattlEye is kernel-level anti-cheat software, which means it runs with the highest level of access on a Windows PC. It is designed to detect and block attempts to use cheating software, manipulate protected game code, and sabotage other players. It is used by Destiny 2, Fortnite, Rainbow Six Siege, Arma 3 and Escape from Tarkov, among others. It can also issue hardware bans, so a cheater who is caught cannot simply make a new account and keep playing on the same PC.

Rockstar built in an escape hatch for single-player fans. An option in the Rockstar Games Launcher lets you disable BattlEye for Story Mode play, and the -nobattleye launch argument does the same thing on third-party launchers. If you try to load into GTA Online with BattlEye disabled, you get an error. Single-player mods need BattlEye switched off, which is why the toggle exists. Steam Deck players get the short end: Rockstar says the Deck does not support BattlEye for GTA Online, so you can play Story Mode but not GTA Online.

Why GTA 6 Online will need it more

GTA Online on PC spent nine years as one of the worst places in gaming for cheaters. Mod menus handed out free money, spawned objects on other players, and crashed lobbies, and Rockstar had no official anti-cheat to stop it. BattlEye did not end cheating, but it raised the cost of it, and cheat makers had to go back to the drawing board.

GTA 6 Online will be a bigger target still. Rockstar has not set a date for the online mode on any platform yet, so there is no PC date for it either, but the audience will be enormous from day one, and the launch window is when anti-cheat matters most. A PC launch without protection would repeat the GTA 5 story, and Rockstar knows it. Our GTA 6 Online on PC page tracks what is known about the mode so far.

The timing also fits Rockstar's current mood. The studio is in an active enforcement phase over the leaked Xbox build and the unofficial PC port projects, with takedowns landing within days of the leak. Our crackdown report covers that in detail. A company that moves that fast to protect its game is unlikely to ship its next online mode without protection.

What we do not know

Rockstar has said nothing about GTA 6 on PC at all: no date, no store page, no system requirements, and no anti-cheat. BattlEye is the likely candidate because Rockstar already licenses it, already built the launcher toggle for it, and already runs it at GTA Online scale. But likely is not confirmed. Rockstar could pick a different solution, build an in-house system, or add anti-cheat later, exactly as it did with GTA 5, which waited nine years.

Do not treat any site claiming a confirmed GTA 6 anti-cheat as a source. Until Rockstar posts patch notes or a Newswire update, every claim about GTA 6 PC anti-cheat is a guess, including this article's expectation that BattlEye returns.

There is also a real trade-off that players will argue about if BattlEye does return. Kernel-level access is the highest level of access a program can have on a Windows PC, and some players do not want any game running at that level. Rockstar's answer in GTA 5 was the Story Mode toggle, and that is probably the shape of the compromise again: mandatory for online play, optional for the story.

What it would mean for mods and Steam Deck

For modders, the GTA 5 model is the best guide we have. If BattlEye is mandatory for GTA 6 Online and switchable for Story Mode, single-player modding survives with the toggle, as it does in GTA 5 today. FiveM-style roleplay servers are a separate question, because they run on their own infrastructure, and our GTA 6 mods and FiveM guide covers that side.

For Steam Deck owners, the news is less good. GTA 5's BattlEye does not support GTA Online on the Deck, so Deck players are limited to Story Mode. If GTA 6 PC uses BattlEye the same way, expect the same limit, at least at launch. Our Steam Deck page tracks that question.

There is one more angle that matters if GTA 6 Online ever gets crossplay. Anti-cheat parity decides whether PC cheaters can reach console lobbies, and that is a common fear whenever crossplay is discussed. Rockstar has not confirmed crossplay for GTA 6 Online either, and our crossplay page covers what is known there.

Quick answers

Will GTA 6 PC use BattlEye?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has announced no GTA 6 PC anti-cheat at all. BattlEye is the likely choice because GTA Online on PC already uses it, but that is an expectation, not a fact.

When did GTA Online get BattlEye?

September 17, 2024, in title update 1.69 for the PC version of GTA 5 and GTA Online.

Is BattlEye mandatory?

For GTA Online on PC, yes. For Story Mode you can switch it off in the Rockstar Games Launcher or with the -nobattleye launch argument.

Can BattlEye ban your hardware?

Yes, it can issue hardware bans, so making a new account does not get a banned cheater back in on the same PC.

Will BattlEye block GTA 6 mods?

Only for online play. In GTA 5, single-player mods work with BattlEye switched off for Story Mode.

Does BattlEye work on Steam Deck?

Not for GTA Online. Steam Deck players can play GTA 5 Story Mode only, with no access to GTA Online.
