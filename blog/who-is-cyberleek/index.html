Who is Cyberleek? The GTA 6 leakers, explained

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

Cyberleek went from an obscure Telegram channel to the most talked-about name in GTA 6 news in a few months. Here's what's actually known about the group, and what isn't.

Short answerCyberleek is an anonymous, financially motivated group that has leaked GTA 6 material since August 2026 and tied its leaks to a Solana memecoin called CYBERLEEK. Nobody behind it has been publicly identified. Critics say the leaks look more like a crypto pump than a mission to bring GTA 6 to PC.

What we know

  • Anonymous. As of late 2026, no one behind Cyberleek has been publicly identified or charged.
  • Financially motivated. Security firm Bitdefender has described it as an extortion-style group, with a Telegram channel dating back to late 2024.
  • Leaking GTA 6 since August 2026. Reports say it began posting unreleased GTA 6 footage around August 18, 2026.
  • Tied to a token. The group promotes a memecoin on the Solana blockchain called CYBERLEEK, and has repeatedly linked releases to the token's market value.

The token pattern

Coverage of Cyberleek keeps coming back to the same pattern: announce a leak, tie it to a market-cap target for the token, and let the hype push buyers in.

WhenWhat happened
Late August 2026Blockchain investigators reportedly traced about $270,000 moving out of wallets linked to the group.
October 2026The group reportedly promised a build with the full story mode if the token hit a $30 million market cap, then said on October 14 that it wouldn't release a build after missing the target.
November 2026It posted a ~209 GB Xbox build, set a $15 million target for releasing the executable, and said it would release it on November 23 regardless.
November 23, 2026The executable was released. Modders got the game to its settings screens on PC within hours. See our boot report.

Why critics call it a pump

Several outlets, including Decrypt, have described the token-linked leaks as a pump: hype drives the price up, and early holders can sell into it. Observers have warned about a possible "rug pull," where the people behind a token cash out and disappear. There's no official finding that the token is a scam, but there's also no link to Rockstar or Take-Two, and Rockstar has never acknowledged it.

Don't buy leak tokens. Memecoins tied to hype are extremely risky, and tokens like this can lose most of their value overnight. Nothing about buying one gets you GTA 6 on PC.

Is Cyberleek behind the 2022 leak?

No. The famous September 2022 leak of GTA 6 development videos was carried out by Arion Kurtaj, a member of the Lapsus$ group, who was later sentenced in the UK to an indefinite hospital order. Cyberleek is a separate, later group. Our leaks timeline covers both.

What happens next?

Since the executable release, Rockstar has reportedly moved against port projects and leak communities, and the Leonida project shut down. See our crackdown report. Whatever Cyberleek does next, the safe way to play GTA 6 on PC is still the official release, reported for early 2027.
