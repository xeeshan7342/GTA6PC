Will GTA 6 come to GeForce Now? The cloud gaming picture on PC

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

If you were hoping to play GTA 6 on a weak PC through the cloud, the launch news is mixed. The game does stream through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but Microsoft and Rockstar limited it to Xbox consoles. GeForce Now, the service most PC players would turn to first, has no Rockstar games in its catalog at all.

Short answerThere is no way to play GTA 6 through cloud gaming on a PC right now. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams it only to Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles, and GeForce Now lists no Rockstar games. The only cloud-style option on PC is Remote Play from a console you already own.

Xbox Cloud Gaming has GTA 6, but not for PC

Rockstar confirmed on November 10, 2026 that GTA 6 is available through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, from launch day, November 19, starting at 1 a.m. ET. The streamed version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings, so it looks a little worse than the game running natively on an Xbox Series X. The game is not included with Game Pass, so you still have to buy it separately, and you need Game Pass cloud time or purchased cloud playtime on top of that.

Game Pass Ultimate includes 15 hours of cloud play a month, and extra hours can be bought, with 50 hours costing about $12. That is the only official way to play GTA 6 without a current-generation console, and it works because Microsoft runs the game on its own servers and streams the picture to the older hardware.

PC players are left out of that deal on purpose. Microsoft restricted GTA 6 cloud streaming to Xbox consoles, so Windows PCs, smartphones, smart TVs and web browsers are excluded at launch. Dexerto reports that Xbox executive Matthew Ball confirmed the game will not stream to PC. That makes GTA 6 an exception, because Xbox Cloud Gaming normally does stream to Windows computers. Our Game Pass guide covers the subscription side in detail.

GeForce Now: no Rockstar games at all

GeForce Now works differently from Xbox Cloud Gaming. You stream games you already own on Steam or the Epic Games Store, and NVIDIA runs them on its servers. But NVIDIA can only stream a game if the publisher allows it, and Rockstar does not allow it. GTA 5 was pulled from GeForce Now in February 2020, and as of November 19, 2026, no Rockstar game is in the GeForce Now catalog at all.

That matters for the future as much as for today. Even when the official GTA 6 PC version arrives, GeForce Now support is not automatic. Rockstar and NVIDIA would need an agreement first, and Rockstar has not had one for any game in more than six years. Until Rockstar opts in, searching GeForce Now for GTA 6 will come up empty, and that is a licensing decision, not a technical problem with your PC or your connection.

Rockstar also runs its own store and launcher, which gives it less reason to hand its biggest game to another company's streaming service. Nothing in Rockstar's announcements so far suggests that is about to change.

What about Boosteroid and PS Plus Premium?

Boosteroid is the one cloud service that does stream GTA 5 on PC. It is a cloud PC: you sign in to Steam, Epic, the Rockstar Games Launcher or the Microsoft Store, and the game runs on its server. But it can only stream games that exist on PC, so it offers GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, not GTA 6. It is a route for playing GTA 5 on a weak PC, not a route to GTA 6.

PlayStation Plus Premium is no help either. GTA 5 left the PS Plus Extra and Premium catalog on November 17, 2026, and the service only streams to a PS5 or a PlayStation Portal anyway. GTA 6 is not on PS Plus Premium cloud streaming at launch. For PlayStation, Rockstar confirmed only Remote Play from your own console, so that is also the only way to play it on a PlayStation Portal.

The one cloud-style option that works on PC today: Remote Play

Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 fully supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. That streams the game from your own console to your PC, TV or phone, and it is the only legitimate way to see GTA 6 on a PC screen right now. It is not cloud gaming, because the game still runs on hardware you own, and it does not help if you do not own a console.

It also needs a solid connection. Our Remote Play guide walks through the setup, and our lag fix guide covers what to do when the stream stutters. Both assume you already have a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S with the game installed.

What would have to change

Two separate decisions would have to go the PC player's way. First, Rockstar would need to opt in to GeForce Now, or another cloud service, for GTA 6. Second, Microsoft would need to lift the console-only restriction on GTA 6 in Xbox Cloud Gaming. Neither has been announced, and neither is likely before the official PC version exists. A GamesBeat report says Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027 for the PC release, but Rockstar has not confirmed it, and our release date page tracks that story.

There is one longer-term sign worth watching. As part of a 10-year partnership with Take-Two, other Rockstar titles like GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 are due to get Xbox Cloud Gaming support over the coming months. That shows the relationship is warming up, but it says nothing about GTA 6 on PC, and it says nothing about GeForce Now.

Until something official changes, treat any site, app or browser page that claims to stream GTA 6 to your PC as fake. There is no PC version and no PC cloud stream, so every download behind those claims is a scam. Our download scams guide explains the risks in detail.

Quick answers

Can I play GTA 6 on GeForce Now?

No. GeForce Now has no Rockstar games in its catalog, and GTA 6 has no PC version yet. Rockstar pulled GTA 5 from the service in February 2020 and has not returned.

Can I stream GTA 6 to my PC with Xbox Cloud Gaming?

No. Microsoft limited GTA 6 cloud streaming to Xbox consoles at launch, so Windows PCs, phones, smart TVs and browsers are excluded.

Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Yes, through Xbox Cloud Gaming on the console itself. You need to buy the game separately and have Game Pass cloud time or purchased cloud playtime.

Is there any way to play GTA 6 on PC without a console?

No, not legitimately. Remote Play streams the game from a console you own to your PC, but it still needs that console.

Will GTA 6 come to GeForce Now when the PC version launches?

Unknown. Rockstar would need to opt in to GeForce Now, and it has not done so for any game since 2020. Nothing has been announced.
