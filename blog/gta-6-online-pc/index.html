GTA 6 Online on PC: release date and what we know

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 25, 2026

GTA 6 launched as a single-player game. The online mode that made GTA V a decade-long hit hasn't arrived yet, on any platform.

Short answerGTA 6 Online has no release date. Bloomberg reported in October 2026 that it is expected in 2027, but Rockstar has not announced it. The game launched on November 19, 2026 as a single-player experience, with more than 39 million players in the first four days on PS5 alone. PC players will need the PC version first, which is reported for early 2027.

GTA 6 launched without online

When GTA 6 arrived on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026, it was a single-player game. Rockstar hasn't officially announced an online mode, let alone a date for one.

What the reports say

  • Bloomberg: in an October 14, 2026 report, Jason Schreier wrote that GTA 6's online component is expected in 2027. Coverage noted that Bloomberg said "expected," not "confirmed."
  • Twitch's CEO told Bloomberg in September 2026 that the multiplayer mode would arrive "next year," without giving a date.
  • A later Bloomberg video suggested online would come about a year after launch. Schreier clarified that this wasn't a confirmed date.

Put together, 2027 looks likely, but nothing is official.

What it means for PC players

PC players need the PC version of the game first. A GamesBeat report says that's targeting the first quarter of 2027. If both reports are right, PC players may not miss much online time at all, because the online mode could land after the PC version.

When GTA V came to PC in 2015, players could carry their GTA Online characters and progress over from PS3 and Xbox 360. Rockstar hasn't said whether anything like that will happen for GTA 6.

What happens to GTA Online?

Take-Two has said it will keep supporting GTA Online after GTA 6 launches, without giving a timeline. For now, GTA Online on PC is still the place to play Rockstar's online world.

What about roleplay servers?

Many PC players spend their GTA time on roleplay servers rather than GTA Online. Rockstar bought the team behind FiveM in 2023, which hints at plans for GTA 6 roleplay. We cover that in GTA 6 mods and FiveM on PC.

What changed since launch

The launch itself was enormous, and it stayed single-player. PlayStation Blog's November 23 launch post says more than 39 million unique players played GTA 6 on PlayStation 5, logging 683 million hours in the launch window from November 19 to November 22, a PlayStation record for concurrent players. All of that play was in the story mode. No online mode appeared at launch, and Rockstar has announced none since.

GTA Online, meanwhile, is still running its weekly events and bonuses. The first bonus week after GTA 6's release has already gone live, and the latest major update, The Kortz Center Heist, came out on July 14, 2026. If you want to play a GTA online mode on PC today, the current GTA Online is still the only option, and it is playable on PC.

Hints in the game files

Nothing below comes from Rockstar, and none of it gives a date. But several finds point the same way, and they are worth knowing about as long as they are labelled as hints, not announcements.

On November 9, 2026, the game's PlayStation database entry was found to include the strings joinSession, launchActivity and launchMultiplayerActivity. Those are the kind of labels a game uses for online sessions, so the find suggests online features are in the works. It says nothing about a date, and database strings are not a product announcement. Insiders who looked at the GTA 6 install also report that the story portion installs separately, which some read as a hint at a separate online module. Rockstar has not announced anything like that, and a separate install could have other explanations, such as download size management. The game's ESRB rating has no "Users Interact" notice, the label given to games where players can communicate with each other.

Crossplay and cross-progression

Rockstar has said nothing about crossplay between PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and with no online mode announced there is nothing to cross yet. The same goes for cross-progression: no statement, no announced transfer of saves or characters between platforms. Rockstar has never shipped crossplay in any of its online games. GTA Online has no crossplay between any of its platforms, and neither does Red Dead Online or Max Payne 3. If GTA 6 Online does arrive, expect it to start on consoles only, with no crossplay, and for the PC question to wait until Rockstar announces a PC version.

The GTA+ month that comes with digital pre-orders is not a GTA 6 online pass, and it does not imply an online mode is coming. It is a subscription benefit for the existing GTA Online. Rockstar has also not said whether characters, money or vehicles from GTA Online will carry over to a future GTA 6 online mode, so there is no official word that a transfer is planned.

Last updated November 25, 2026. We update this page when Rockstar announces anything new.
