Will GTA 6 on PC support DLSS and FSR?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

Nobody can confirm DLSS or FSR support for GTA 6 on PC yet, because Rockstar has not announced a PC version at all. The best evidence is Rockstar's most recent PC release. GTA 5 Enhanced arrived on PC in March 2025 with Nvidia DLSS 3, AMD FSR 1 and 3, and Intel XeSS built in, and that tells us a lot about what to expect when GTA 6 eventually reaches PC.

Short answerDLSS and FSR support for GTA 6 on PC is not confirmed, because no PC version has been announced. But Rockstar's most recent PC release, GTA 5 Enhanced (March 2025), shipped with Nvidia DLSS 3, AMD FSR 1 and 3, and Intel XeSS. If GTA 6 follows the same pattern, all three upscalers should be there on day one.

The short answer: not confirmed, but likely

Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6, no PC date and no PC graphics features. The game launched on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A GamesBeat report from October 2026 says a PC release is planned for the first quarter of 2027, citing anonymous industry sources, but Rockstar has not confirmed it. So any statement that GTA 6 on PC will support DLSS or FSR is a prediction, not a fact. What we can do is look at what Rockstar actually shipped last time, and that evidence is strong. Our release date page tracks the PC announcement story.

Rockstar's most recent PC release is GTA 5 Enhanced, which arrived on PC on March 4, 2025 as a free upgrade. It brought the PC version in line with the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions and added a set of PC specific features. Among them were ray traced ambient occlusion and global illumination, faster loading with SSD and DirectStorage, DualSense controller support with adaptive triggers, and support for AMD FSR 1 and FSR 3, Nvidia DLSS 3, and Intel XeSS. Rockstar also said frame generation support for DLSS and FSR was planned for a future date. That is the feature set Rockstar now treats as standard for a PC release, and it is the best guide to GTA 6.

What each upscaler does

DLSS, FSR and XeSS all solve the same problem in different ways. They render the game at a lower internal resolution than your monitor, then rebuild the image to full resolution using either AI models or spatial techniques. The result is a higher frame rate with an image that is close to native quality, and in DLSS Quality mode it can look better than native rendering in motion. For a game as demanding as GTA 6 is expected to be, with a huge open world, dense traffic and ray traced lighting, upscaling is the feature that will decide whether mid range cards can hold 60 frames per second at 1440p.

DLSS is Nvidia only and runs on RTX cards. GTA 5 Enhanced shipped with DLSS 3, including DLAA, the native resolution anti-aliasing mode that uses the same AI model without upscaling. FSR is AMD's open alternative and runs on almost any modern GPU, including Nvidia and Intel cards. GTA 5 Enhanced shipped with FSR 1 and FSR 3, and independent testing found FSR 3 Quality close to DLSS Quality, with DLSS ahead on fine detail in motion. XeSS is Intel's upscaler, and it also runs on any modern GPU. Having all three means no player is locked out by their card brand.

Why upscaling matters so much for GTA 6

GTA 6 is the most demanding game Rockstar has made. The console versions run at 30 frames per second in their quality modes, and the open world of Leonida is far denser than Los Santos. Our ray tracing page covers the lighting side: GTA 5 Enhanced added ray traced global illumination and ambient occlusion on PC, and GTA 6 is expected to push that further. Ray tracing is expensive, and upscaling is the tool that pays for it. Without DLSS or FSR, a large share of PC players would have to choose between ray tracing and a smooth frame rate.

The performance picture from GTA 5 Enhanced shows how much headroom upscaling buys. Testing by Wccftech found the game running at 123 frames per second on average in free roam on a high end rig without frame generation, and noted that DLSS Multi Frame Generation could potentially triple that result once supported. That was a twelve year old game. GTA 6 will be heavier, so the same tools will matter more, not less. Our graphics card guide and system requirements page cover the hardware side in more detail.

What is still unconfirmed

Three things are still open. First, whether GTA 6 on PC ships with DLSS 4 rather than DLSS 3. GTA 5 Enhanced launched with DLSS 3, and DLSS 4 with Multi Frame Generation arrived after it, so a 2027 GTA 6 PC release would more likely ship with the newer version. Second, whether frame generation is included at launch or added later, as Rockstar planned for GTA 5 Enhanced. Third, whether Rockstar keeps its habit of routing the game through the Rockstar Games Launcher, which has caused a real annoyance in GTA 5 Enhanced: the launcher replaces an updated DLSS file with the default version, so players who update DLSS manually need a workaround. None of this is confirmed, and none of it can be confirmed until Rockstar announces the PC version.

One more caution: do not download anything that claims to add DLSS or FSR to GTA 6 on PC today. There is no PC version, so there is nothing to add it to. What to plan for

If you are buying a graphics card with GTA 6 on PC in mind, an RTX card buys you DLSS, and a modern AMD or Intel card buys you FSR and XeSS. Any card from the last few generations will have at least one upscaler available on day one if Rockstar repeats the GTA 5 Enhanced feature set. That is a reasonable expectation, not a promise. The announcement to watch for is the PC version itself. When Rockstar announces it, the feature list will follow, and this page will be updated with the confirmed upscaler support.

Quick answers

Will GTA 6 on PC support DLSS?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has announced no PC version. But GTA 5 Enhanced on PC shipped with Nvidia DLSS 3, so DLSS support is likely.

Will GTA 6 on PC support FSR?

Not confirmed. GTA 5 Enhanced on PC shipped with AMD FSR 1 and FSR 3, so FSR support is likely on the same basis.

Will GTA 6 on PC support frame generation?

Unknown. Rockstar said frame generation support was planned for GTA 5 Enhanced at a future date, but it had not shipped at launch in March 2025.

Does GTA 6 on PC need DLSS or FSR?

No, but both help a lot. Upscalers raise frame rates at 1440p and 4K, which matters in a demanding open world game with ray tracing.

Will GTA 6 on PC support Intel XeSS?

Not confirmed, but likely. GTA 5 Enhanced shipped with Intel XeSS alongside DLSS and FSR, and XeSS runs on any modern GPU.
