GTA 6 PC port update, Nov 28: Belobug removed, CGBJ vanishes and AI agents take over

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 28, 2026

The day in short

November 28 was the strangest day yet in the race to get GTA 6 running on PC. GitHub removed Belobug, the leading unofficial build, and its author account with it. A different project, CGBJ, went public and vanished within the hour. And the reporting that explains the pace of it all landed: more than 40 unofficial porting projects spun up in four days, with AI coding agents doing much of the reverse engineering. Through it all, the best credible frame rate is still about 20 FPS, and nothing is stable or safe to download.

The sales backdrop makes the urgency easy to understand. Alinea Analytics estimates GTA 6 sold 53 million copies in its first week, worth about $4.7 billion, all of it on consoles. There is still no official PC version, and the GamesBeat report pointing to the first quarter of 2027 is still unconfirmed. Our first week sales report has the full figures. Until Rockstar announces a PC version, the port race is the only route to a PC screen, and it is living on borrowed time.

Belobug removed: the leading build is gone

The biggest story of the day is a removal. Belobug, the project that had become the frontrunner in the port race, is gone from GitHub, and so is its author account. Both now return 404 errors. PortWatch VI, the tracker site following the race, reported the removal as a Terms of Service action on November 27 at 18:33 UTC.

Belobug mattered because it was the furthest along by the tracker's own measure, at 91.3 percent complete, and because it was the project other builders were watching. PCQuest reported that it needed about 30 GB of RAM and ran at around 22 FPS. Our full report has the timeline and what the removal means for the projects built on its work: Belobug GTA 6 PC port removed. Whether the removal was a DMCA notice from Take-Two or a GitHub Terms of Service action is not confirmed, and GitHub publishes takedown notices days after the event, so the paperwork may surface later.

CGBJ went public, then vanished in under an hour

The second story is a disappearance. CGBJ, a private build based on the VIctory project, went public in the early hours of November 28 and was gone within the hour. iPlayGTA6 reported it at 04:05 UTC, by which time the repository had already gathered 293 stars and 82 forks. The project claimed 25 FPS, against a developer log showing 17.2 to 18.5 FPS, and nobody has confirmed why it vanished. A takedown is possible, and so is a voluntary deletion by the author. Our full report covers both possibilities: CGBJ, the GTA 6 PC port that went public, then vanished.

CGBJ is the second project in two days to disappear after reaching the public, following Belobug. The pattern is now the story: the further a project gets, the faster it attracts attention, and the faster it goes dark.

AI coding agents are now doing the work

The third story explains why the race is moving so fast. The Decoder reported on November 27 that more than 40 unofficial GTA 6 PC porting projects spun up within four days of the Xbox build leaking, with developers handing much of the reverse engineering to AI coding agents, including Anthropic's Claude Code. Nearly half of the projects are bootable, the furthest is past 90 percent complete on the tracker's scale, and footage of an unofficial multiplayer mode is running at 25 to 30 FPS.

Take-Two's response so far has been a GitHub DMCA takedown that pulled an initial repository and more than 3,000 forks, and the company has since moved to strengthen its legal defences against AI built clones, according to TweakTown. Our full report has the details: AI coding agents are building GTA 6 PC ports. One timing detail matters. The Decoder published at 15:37 UTC on November 27, and Belobug was removed at 18:33 UTC the same day, so the report describes the race as it stood hours before the leading build disappeared.

30 FPS, but only in the boat scene

The fourth story is a frame rate claim that needs the same care as every other one this week. Reddit user u/Rima_Mashiro-Hina posted footage of the XCL port running at 30 FPS and above in the opening boat scene, and Twisted Voxel and Polygon both reported it on November 26. A second build, from Group BoYoo, averaged 27 FPS on a boat mission, with the minimum dropping to 21 or 22 FPS.

Both clips show light open water scenes, shader stutter is still visible, and performance in the city is untested. The independent tracker still puts the best credible frame rate at about 20 FPS, with 6 to 13 FPS in the city. Our full report explains the gap between a clip and a measurement: GTA 6 PC port hits 30 FPS. A PC build that holds 30 FPS in Vice City at rush hour has not been shown yet, by anyone.

Where the race stands

Pulling the day together, the race has lost its two furthest projects in about 36 hours, and gained a new explanation for its speed. Belobug is gone, CGBJ is gone, and ScarlettPort, the only public release, is still the furthest a port has come, at about 20 FPS on an RX 9060 XT. The AI agent story suggests replacements will keep appearing, but the Belobug and CGBJ removals suggest they will keep disappearing too. Take-Two's November 24 takedown of 3,007 repositories is still in effect, and no new takedown notice has appeared in GitHub's archive since, though the archive publishes notices days late.

The honest summary of November 28 is the same as November 27, with the names changed. The ports are real, they are improving fast, they keep vanishing, and none of them is a game you can play yet.

Fakes are still the biggest risk

The most important fact of the day has nothing to do with frame rates. A build claiming to be CGBJ is already on torrent sites, and DSOGaming advises avoiding it, since torrent sites are currently filled with fake GTA 6 PC torrents. Most files going around as a GTA 6 PC download carry password-stealing malware, and none of the real projects distributes a finished game.

The rule has not changed since launch day. If a site offers you a complete GTA 6 PC download, a single installer, or a torrent of the full game, it is a fake. The real projects contain no game assets and need a copy of the game to run at all. Do not download anything, do not pay anyone, and do not enter your passwords anywhere that promises early access. Rockstar has announced no PC version, and the GamesBeat report pointing to the first quarter of 2027 remains unconfirmed. When there is a real PC version, Rockstar will announce it, and we will cover it here.

What to watch next

Three things matter now. First, whether ScarlettPort survives, now that the two projects around it have gone dark. Second, whether the 30 FPS boat scene claim is followed by a city test with real measurements. Third, whether the AI agent projects produce a public build that lasts longer than an hour. Any one of those could change the picture overnight, and we will update this page when it happens.
