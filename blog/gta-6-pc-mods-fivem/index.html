Will GTA 6 have mods and roleplay servers on PC?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

On PC, GTA V lived a second life through mods and roleplay servers. GTA 6 on PC could go even further, because Rockstar now owns the biggest roleplay platform.

Short answerRockstar hasn't announced mod support for GTA 6. But in 2023 Rockstar bought Cfx.re, the team behind the FiveM and RedM roleplay platforms, which strongly suggests roleplay will be part of GTA 6's future on PC.

Why PC matters for mods

Consoles don't allow mods for GTA. On PC, GTA V became a platform for graphics overhauls, new cars, new missions and, most of all, roleplay servers where players act out life in Los Santos. Roleplay streams have been some of the most-watched GTA content on Twitch for years.

Rockstar bought FiveM's team

In August 2023, Rockstar announced that Cfx.re, the team behind FiveM for GTA V and RedM for Red Dead Redemption 2, had officially joined Rockstar. Those platforms run the custom multiplayer servers that most GTA roleplay happens on.

Rockstar said the move was about supporting the roleplay community. Nobody buys a team like that for a game it's about to retire, so it's widely seen as a sign that roleplay will matter for GTA 6.

What we don't know

  • Whether GTA 6 will support mods at all on PC.
  • Whether there will be an official roleplay mode, a FiveM-style platform, or both.
  • How Rockstar will handle mods that clash with GTA 6 Online once it launches.

Be careful with "GTA 6 mods" today

Since there's no PC version yet, any "GTA 6 mod" you find right now is either a GTA V mod using GTA 6 names, or a scam. Don't download "GTA 6 mod packs" that claim to unlock the game on PC. See our scam guide.

When will we know more?

Likely when Rockstar announces the PC version, which a GamesBeat report says is targeting early 2027. Check our release date page for updates.
