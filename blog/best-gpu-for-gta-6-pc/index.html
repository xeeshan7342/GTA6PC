Best graphics cards for GTA 6 on PC

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

The graphics card is the single biggest factor in how GTA 6 will run on PC. Rockstar hasn't published requirements, but the consoles give us a solid baseline. Here are our picks by resolution.

Short answerThese are estimates, since Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements. For 1080p, aim for an RTX 4060/5060 or RX 7600/9060 XT class card. For 1440p, an RTX 4070 Super/5070 or RX 9070 class card. For 4K, an RTX 5080 or better. 12 GB+ of VRAM is the safest bet.

The console baseline

GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on every console. The base PS5 renders at around 1440p with dips into the 20s, and the PS5 Pro adds upscaling and ray-traced shadows. The PS5's GPU is roughly comparable to a mid-range desktop card from a few years ago, and PC players usually want more than 30 FPS. So GTA 6 on PC will likely need a stronger card than people expect. Why VRAM matters

GTA 6 streams a huge, detailed world, and modern open-world games often use a lot of video memory at high texture settings. Cards with 8 GB of VRAM may need lower texture settings. 12 GB or more is a safer choice if you're buying now, and 16 GB gives the most headroom.

Upscaling will be important

Expect upscaling like NVIDIA DLSS, AMD FSR or Intel XeSS to play a big role, just as PSSR and FSR do on the PS5 Pro and Xbox Series S. Rockstar hasn't confirmed which upscalers the PC version will support. More in our ray tracing and DLSS article.

Should you buy a GPU now?

If your current card is struggling with today's games, upgrading to a 12–16 GB card is a reasonable move. If not, wait. The PC version is reported for early 2027, official requirements should arrive before then, and GPU prices change often.
