GTA 6 PC leak explained: what's real and what isn't

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Around the console launch, a group called Cyberleek said it had put a near-complete Xbox build of GTA 6 online. Since then the internet has been full of "GTA 6 on PC" claims. Here's what's actually going on.

Short answerCyberleek says it released a ~209 GB Xbox build of GTA 6, labeled version 1.0.2.1, but held back the main executable. The files haven't been fully verified, no one has shown it running on PC, and a viral "PC port" video turned out to be GTA V footage.

What happened

Around November 19, 2026, the hacker group Cyberleek said it had published what it called a near-complete GTA 6 Xbox build, about 209 GB in size. Reports say the build is labeled as Xbox version 1.0.2.1.

The group held back one key file: the game's main executable. It tied releasing that file to the market value of its own crypto token, and later said it would release it on November 23 regardless. As of this writing, there's no confirmed report that a working executable is out.

Is the leak real?

Partly checked, not confirmed. Some people in the hacking community have looked through the files and say they appear genuine. Nobody has fully verified the build, and Rockstar hasn't commented on it.

Does this mean GTA 6 is on PC?

No. A console build isn't a PC game. Cyberleek claimed modders could get it running on PC within a week, but there are big obstacles:

  • The game's files are reported to be encrypted, with the key tied to the withheld executable.
  • The build relies on Xbox-specific system functions that Windows doesn't have.
  • No working Xbox Series X|S emulator exists, and community projects are at a very early stage. GTA 6 doesn't boot in any of them.

We go deeper on this in Can the leaked Xbox build run on PC?

The fake "GTA 6 PC port" video

One widely shared clip claimed to show GTA 6 running on PC. XDA Developers found it was a hoax put together from recycled GTA V footage. Expect more fakes like this while people are searching for GTA 6 on PC.

The real risk: malware and scams

Don't download "GTA 6 PC" files, installers or "ports." Fake installers and phishing pages are circulating alongside the leak. Leaked game files are also a common way to spread malware, and downloading or sharing them can carry legal risk.

We don't link to leaked files, and we won't.

GTA 6 has leaked before

This isn't the first big GTA 6 leak. In September 2022, more than 90 early development videos were posted online. Rockstar confirmed that leak and said it was "extremely disappointed." The person responsible was later dealt with by the UK courts. Cyberleek also claimed earlier GTA 6 leaks before this one.

What PC players should do

Wait for Rockstar's official PC version. A GamesBeat report says it's targeting early 2027. See our GTA 6 PC release date page for the latest.
