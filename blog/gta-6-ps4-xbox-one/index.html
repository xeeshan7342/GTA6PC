Can you play GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

Millions of players still use a PS4 or Xbox One. Unfortunately, GTA 6 wasn't made for them. Here's what your options are.

Short answerNo. GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no PS4 or Xbox One version, and buying it won't make it run on an older console. Xbox One owners can reportedly stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. On PC, the official version is reported for early 2027.

Why there's no PS4 or Xbox One version

Rockstar's GTA 6 page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Sony's page says the game won't be available on PS4. GTA 6's huge, dense open world relies on the newer consoles' faster processors and SSDs. Even the PS5 and Xbox Series X run it at 30 FPS, so the older machines would struggle badly.

Option 1: Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One

Reports say Xbox Cloud Gaming streams GTA 6 to Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles. The game runs on Microsoft's servers, and your Xbox One just shows the video, so you can play without new hardware. You'll need a fast, stable internet connection and the right Game Pass tier, so check Xbox's current requirements first.

Note that cloud streaming of GTA 6 to PCs, browsers and phones hasn't been offered.

Option 2: Upgrade your console

A PS5 or Xbox Series X|S is the straightforward way to play now. The Xbox Series S is the cheapest option, but it's the weakest version of GTA 6, rendering at around 720p before upscaling. See how each console performs.

Option 3: Wait for PC

If you were planning a gaming PC anyway, the official PC version is reported for the first quarter of 2027. Check our estimated PC requirements and PC build guide.

Don't fall for "GTA 6 PS4" downloads

Any site or video offering a "GTA 6 PS4 version" or "Xbox One patch" is fake. These are common scams, the same as fake PC downloads. See our scam guide.
