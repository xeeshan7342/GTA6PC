Can you move a GTA 6 save from PS5 or Xbox to PC?

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 24, 2026

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and many players are already asking the same question: if a PC version arrives, will my story progress come with me? The short answer is no, not through any route Rockstar has announced. Some moves do work today, but only inside the same console family.

Short answerThere is no announced way to transfer a GTA 6 save from PS5 or Xbox to PC. Rockstar has announced no cross-save, no cross-progression and no save migration tool for GTA 6, and there is no PC version to move a save to. Moves inside one console family do work: PS5 to PS5 Pro, and Xbox to Xbox.

The short answer: no route to PC exists

Rockstar has announced no cross-save, no cross-progression and no save migration tool for GTA 6. The game launched on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and there is no PC version to move a save to. A GamesBeat report from October 2026 says a PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027, citing anonymous industry sources, but Rockstar has not confirmed it. Until Rockstar announces both a PC version and a transfer feature, plan on starting over if you switch platform. Our release date page tracks the PC story as it develops.

Your save is tied to your console account, not to your Rockstar Games account. Signing in with the same Rockstar account on another platform does not move your story progress, and buying the game again on another platform does not create a migration right. Any guide that gives you menu steps for a console to PC transfer is inventing them, because no official page describes such a transfer.

PS5 to PS5 Pro, or PS5 to a new PS5: yes

Moving inside the PlayStation family works, because GTA 6 saves are ordinary PS5 saved data and Sony's own tools move them. On your old PS5, go to Settings, then System, then System Software, then Data Transfer, and follow the prompts to copy your saved data to the new console over your local network. The other route is PlayStation Plus cloud storage: upload the GTA 6 save from the old console, then download it on the new one. Both routes move the full story save, including the 15 manual save slots and the autosave slot that reviewers have described.

The same applies to a PS5 Pro. GTA 6 has its own enhanced graphics options on the Pro, and your save carries across with either method above. There is no Rockstar step involved. If the save does not appear on the new console, check that you are signed in with the same PlayStation Network account and that the cloud upload finished before you switched the old console off.

Xbox to Xbox: yes, automatically

On Xbox, there is nothing to do. The Microsoft Store listing for GTA 6 carries the Xbox cloud saves tag, so your progress syncs with your Xbox account. Sign in on another Xbox Series X or Series S, install the game, and it picks up your save where you left off. This works between a Series X and a Series S in either direction, and it works if your old console is lost or sold, as long as your last session synced.

One thing to watch is timing. Cloud saves sync when you quit the game and the console is online. If you switch consoles minutes after a session, give the old console a moment to finish syncing, or check the sync status before you start playing on the new one. Playing on the new console before the sync finishes can create a conflict prompt, and picking the wrong side of that prompt can roll your progress back.

PS5 to Xbox, or Xbox to PS5: no

There is no cross-save between PlayStation and Xbox in GTA 6. Each store sells its own license, and Rockstar has published no tool or cross-save feature for the game. If you buy GTA 6 on PS5 and later buy it again on Xbox, plan on starting the story over. Our crossplay page covers the related multiplayer question, and the answer there is the same shape: nothing has been announced, because the game launched as a single player experience with no online mode yet.

Rockstar did run one-off migration tools for GTA 5 when new console versions arrived, letting eligible profiles move between console generations. Nothing similar exists for GTA 6, and the GTA 5 precedent does not help here anyway, because those tools moved profiles between console generations, never between PlayStation and Xbox at the same generation, and never to PC.

Can you move a GTA 6 save to PC?

No, because there is no PC version. Rockstar lists GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has announced a PC release or a date. The GamesBeat report pointing to the first quarter of 2027 is still unconfirmed, and analyst estimates range wider. If a PC version is announced, a save transfer would still need a separate announcement. Rockstar has never offered one for its biggest games.

The GTA Online record is the clearest guide. Rockstar's transfer policy and PC migration instructions exclude console to PC profile migration in both directions. Eligible profiles can move between older and newer consoles, and eligible players can migrate from GTA Online on PC Legacy to PC Enhanced, but neither route imports a console character to PC. Rockstar's own support article, last updated February 24, 2026, states that progress cannot be migrated between PlayStation 4 or Xbox One and PC. GTA 6 has no online mode announced yet, so there is no GTA 6 Online progress to move, but the policy direction is plain.

What to do now

Pick the console you plan to finish the story on, and treat that as your home for this playthrough. If you own both consoles, play on the one your friends play on, because the save will not follow you if you change your mind. Keep cloud saves switched on: PlayStation Plus cloud storage on PS5, and automatic syncing on Xbox. If you buy a PS5 Pro later, the transfer takes minutes with the steps above.

If a PC version is announced, check Rockstar's support pages before assuming anything about saves. A transfer feature would be announced there, with eligibility rules and a cutoff, the way Rockstar announced the GTA 5 migration tools. Until then, the honest position is that your console save stays on your console. That is the answer today, and this page will be updated if Rockstar announces a PC version or a transfer tool.

Quick answers

Can you transfer a GTA 6 save from PS5 to PC?

No. Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6 and no save transfer route to PC.

Can you transfer a GTA 6 save from Xbox to PC?

No. There is no PC version and no announced migration tool, so a console save cannot move to PC.

Can you move a GTA 6 save from PS5 to a PS5 Pro?

Yes. Use the PS5 Data Transfer feature, or upload the save to PlayStation Plus cloud storage and download it on the new console.

Can you move a GTA 6 save from PS5 to Xbox?

No. There is no cross-save between PlayStation and Xbox in GTA 6.

Will GTA Online progress transfer from console to PC?

No. Rockstar's transfer policy excludes console to PC migration in both directions, and nothing announced changes that for GTA 6.
