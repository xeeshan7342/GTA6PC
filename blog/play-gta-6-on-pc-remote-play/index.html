How to play GTA 6 on PC right now, legally

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 24, 2026

There's no PC version of GTA 6 yet. But if you own a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S, there's an official way to play it on your PC screen today.

Short answerRockstar confirmed GTA 6 supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. If you own the game on a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, you can stream it from your console to a Windows PC. Xbox Cloud Gaming does not stream GTA 6 to PC, and any "PC download" is fake.

What Remote Play is

Remote Play streams a game from your own console to another screen over your home network or the internet. The game still runs on your PS5 or Xbox. Your PC just shows the picture and sends your controller inputs back.

In its November 10, 2026 announcement, Rockstar said GTA 6 fully supports Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox.

What you need

  • A PS5 or Xbox Series X|S that you own, with GTA 6 installed.
  • A Windows PC (the PS Remote Play app also runs on Mac).
  • A controller. A DualSense or Xbox controller connected to the PC works best.
  • A good connection. Wired Ethernet on the console and PC makes a big difference.

Option 1: PS Remote Play from a PS5

  1. On your PS5, turn on Remote Play in the system settings, and set the console to stay connected to the internet in rest mode.
  2. On your PC, download the official PS Remote Play app from PlayStation's website.
  3. Sign in with the same PlayStation account as your PS5, connect your controller, and let the app find your console.
  4. Start GTA 6 from the PS5 menu on your PC screen.

Option 2: Xbox Remote Play from an Xbox Series X|S

  1. On your Xbox, turn on remote features in the device settings.
  2. On your PC, open the Xbox app for Windows. You need Windows 10 or 11, version 22H2 or newer.
  3. Sign in with the same Microsoft account, choose your console, and start streaming.

Remote Play doesn't need Game Pass. You just need to own the game on your console.

What about Xbox Cloud Gaming?

GTA 6 is on Xbox Cloud Gaming, but Microsoft only lets it stream to Xbox consoles, not to PCs, browsers or phones. If a site claims you can cloud-stream GTA 6 to a PC today, it's wrong, at least for now.

What to expect

  • Image quality and input lag depend on your network. It won't look or feel as sharp as playing on the console directly.
  • It's still 30 FPS. The game runs on your console, so you get console performance. More on that in GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on consoles.
  • No mods or PC settings. Those will need the real PC version.

No console? Then you have to wait

If you don't own a PS5 or Xbox, there's no legitimate way to play GTA 6 on PC yet. The official PC version is reported for early 2027. Any site offering a "GTA 6 PC download" is a scam. See our scam guide.
