Has GTA 6 been dumped on PS5? Firmware 14.23 explained

By GTA6 PC EditorialPublished November 23, 2026

Projects like AnyPS5 can run some PS5 games on PC, but only if someone first dumps the game from a jailbroken PS5. For GTA 6, Sony got there first.

Short answerGTA 6 has not been publicly dumped on PS5. Sony released firmware 14.23 on November 17 and made it required for GTA 6, while public PS5 jailbreaks only reach 13.60. One researcher claimed a dump on 14.22 but gave no details.

What "dumping" means

To run a PS5 game on PC through projects like AnyPS5, someone first has to copy the decrypted game off a jailbroken PS5. That's called a dump. Without it, those tools have nothing to work with.

Firmware 14.23 and GTA 6

On November 17, 2026, two days before GTA 6 launched, Sony released PS5 system software 14.23. The patch notes only mention "security fixes." Reports say PlayStation Support confirmed on November 18 that the update is required to play GTA 6, so anyone playing the game has to be on the newest firmware.

Where PS5 jailbreaks are

  • Public jailbreak tools reportedly only work up to firmware 13.60.
  • A security researcher known as Gezine claimed to have dumped a game binary on firmware 14.22, but didn't say which game or share any technical details.
  • Sony's 14.23 update arrived the day after that claim. Sony didn't link the two.

So for GTA 6, which needs 14.23, there's no public way to dump it today.

What it means for GTA 6 on PC

The PS5 route to an unofficial PC version is blocked for now. The other route, through the leaked Xbox build, is also stuck, because it's missing its executable. See Leonida and our Cyberleek update.

Firmware races like this can change quickly, but every patch makes it harder. Meanwhile the official PC version is reported for early 2027.

A note on jailbreaking

Jailbreaking your own console can void your warranty and get you banned from PlayStation Network, and we don't provide jailbreak guides. Downloading dumped games you don't own is piracy.
