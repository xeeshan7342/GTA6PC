About GTA6 PC

GTA6 PC is an independent fan site for people waiting to play Grand Theft Auto VI on PC. We cover the PC release date, official announcements, leaks and the claims going around about GTA 6 on PC.

How we write

  • We separate what Rockstar has confirmed from reports and rumors, and we say which is which.
  • Every article links to its sources.
  • We never link to leaked game files or unofficial downloads.
  • We update articles when the facts change and show the date of the last update.

Editorial policy

  • Confirmed vs reported: we only call something confirmed when Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft has said it. Everything else is labeled as a report, a rumor or our estimate.
  • Sources: we link to the original reporting, and we prefer established outlets and official announcements.
  • Images: cover graphics are made by us. Official artwork is credited to Rockstar Games and used for news and commentary.
  • No piracy: we never link to leaked builds, game files, cracks or jailbreak tools, and we warn readers about fake downloads.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and update the article's date. Send corrections to contact@gta6pc.buzz with the article link.

Independence

GTA6 PC isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. The site is supported by advertising, and advertisers have no say over what we write.
